Where this feed goes

  • Opponent-aware ratings. A rating that never sees the score cannot be opponent-aware; the score here comes from the game's own document, not from a table of standings.
  • Backtests and ROI. A pre-registered strategy can only be scored against a settled result, and the settled result must be traceable to an official source with a fetch time. This table is that source.
  • Venue disagreements. Where the game centre and the Game Book print different venue text the two columns are published side by side and the difference is flagged for the review page; it is not resolved by picking one.
