How to read this, and how not to

  • The rate is P(appears on the club's game-day inactive list | designation). It is a real, officially sourced number and it is not the same thing as the probability that the player plays.
  • An Out designation already makes a player ineligible under the league's own report, whether or not the coach spends one of the eight inactive designations on him. So a rate far below 100% for Out is what the sources produce, and it does not mean Out players played.
  • A Questionable player who is not on the inactive list was active. Whether he took a snap is a Game Book fact, not an inactive-list fact, and it stays blank until the Game Book for that game is captured.
  • n is small per designation per week. Any model that consumes these numbers must use the interval, and the register publishes it rather than a point estimate.
  • The old convention the brief asks us to stop using - a fixed percentage attached to "Questionable" from historical memory - is replaced by this table, recomputed from captures on every run.
