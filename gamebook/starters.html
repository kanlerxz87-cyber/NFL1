"Actually started" is the Game Book's own line-up line for that game - the first dated official statement of who was on the field at kickoff.

Until a capture exists the table stays empty: the club depth charts already in this repository are not enough to state who started, and no starter is inferred from a jersey number, a printed rank or a salary.

What it will be used for

  • Replacement depth, not replacement fact. A starter being out is one bit; the second bit is who took his place and whether that name was a rank-1 player on the club's own chart or a promotion from further down. That distinction is the difference between pricing a lineup change and guessing at it.
  • Ablations instead of fixed penalties. A model can be re-run with and without the verified availability features on the same chronological holdout; the difference, with its interval, is the number that belongs on the site - not a subtraction of points per missing starter.
  • Deficiency by string. A promoted second- or third-string player is identified by having started a game he was not the rank-1 name for, which is a played fact, not a jersey order.
