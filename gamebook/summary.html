Reading it

  • Blank is an answer. The play-rate column, the two-source cross-check and the expected-versus-actual starter table are blank because the Game Book captures do not exist in the tree yet - not because the measurement came out at zero.
  • Two of the three calibration rows above are about what a designation implies for the inactive list, which is a data-consistency fact. The number a pricing model needs - did he play - is a different measurement and stays empty until a Game Book is stored for the game.
  • The three Trap rows are the reason the availability universe cannot be taken from any single official document, and they are published instead of smoothed.

Where each thread goes

The register is one input to the decision-readiness table on the landing page (all requirements), the open questions (questions) and the suggestions awaiting a decision (suggestions). Those pages are generated by their own builders; this page only links them, so the numbers here are not duplicated into a second place that could disagree.
