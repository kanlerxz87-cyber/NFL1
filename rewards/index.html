Read this first. Two-stage reward rule. Stage 1 (proper-score qualification) awards a Verified Signal badge when a strategy's Brier skill lower bound is > 0, its BH-adjusted p-value is < 0.05 (and it clears Bonferroni 0.05/N_tests), and its decided-bet sample is >= 200, on a frozen walk-forward window. Stage 2 (prospective CLV/ROI escrow) awards the Top Single Bet / Top Parlay / Top Player Prop Combo badge to the rank-1 eligible strategy whose prospective ROI lower bound is > 0. No badge is awarded if no strategy qualifies. This is a pre-registered proposal (policy), frozen before any strategy is graded against it. It is not an official NFL fact: the league publishes no reward programme. The thresholds are taken from this repository's own proper-score method, not invented.

The two stages

Stage 1 — Proper-Score Qualification

A strategy earns the Verified Signal badge when, on a frozen walk-forward out-of-sample window, its Brier skill lower bound is > 0, its BH-adjusted p-value is < 0.05 (and it clears Bonferroni 0.05 / N_tests), and its decided-bet sample is ≥ 200.

Value: 1 qualification point and Stage-2 eligibility in the track.

Stage 2 — Prospective CLV / ROI Escrow

Only Stage-1-qualified strategies enter a registered multi-week prospective escrow. The Top {Track} badge goes to the rank-1 eligible strategy whose prospective ROI lower bound is > 0. If none qualifies, no badge is awarded.

Value: the track badge plus rank 1 in the track. Non-monetary.

Demotion and re-promotion

A badged strategy returns to paper if it trips demotion rule D1-D5 (data/strat/demotion_rules.json). Re-promotion requires a fresh prospective sample of 200 settled bets clearing D1-D3 in the opposite direction.

What would change our mind

Upside — a badge is earned

A strategy earns the Verified Signal badge when its Brier skill lower bound is > 0, its BH-adjusted p-value is < 0.05 (and clears Bonferroni 0.05/N_tests), and its decided-bet sample is >= 200 on a frozen walk-forward window; a track badge follows when its prospective ROI lower bound is > 0 over the registered escrow window.

Downside — a badge is withdrawn

A badge is withdrawn when the skill lower bound falls to <= 0, or the BH-adjusted p-value rises to >= 0.05, or the sample falls below the track floor, or any demotion rule D1-D5 trips.

What this board never publishes

  • Backtest performance numbers. No ROI, win rate, hit rate, profit or rank derived from a backtest appears here. Stage 1 publishes the rule, the thresholds and a qualification status — never a backtest figure. The underlying proper-score result is linked, not re-published.
  • A badge without a cleared Stage-1 gate. No exceptions.
  • A monetary value. Badges are non-monetary by rule.
