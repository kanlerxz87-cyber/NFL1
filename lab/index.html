What this is

A decision lab for NFL lines that are originated here, not borrowed. Ratings are fitted from game results that have been reconciled line by line against the official standings. Rules are scored under ten validation gates, corrected for multiple testing and compared against a random-bet null. Injuries are joined to the clubs' own depth charts so a missing starter is matched with the second, third or fourth string behind them.

The headline finding is negative and useful: no rule can be certified today, because two gates need inputs that do not exist — book prices and a second season. Everything that blocks certification is listed on Flagged for review with its official source.
