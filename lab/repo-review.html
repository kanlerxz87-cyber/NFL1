The one defect that mattered

The first run of the leaderboard reported p-values of 0.99 for strategies with positive returns. The cause was a hand-written Student-t distribution that was wrong in the middle of its range while looking plausible in the tails. It was replaced with scipy's implementation and asserted equal to it at five points. Had it survived, every significance claim on this site would have been inverted.
