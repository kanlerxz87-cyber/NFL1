How to use these numbers

  • A correlation of zero means the product of two leg prices is the right price for the combination. Anything else means it is not.
  • Positive correlation makes a combination worth more than the product, so a book pricing at the product is paying too little.
  • Negative correlation makes it worth less, which is the trap in pairing a team's passing yards with its own rushing yards.
  • These are correlations, not probabilities. They say how two things move together, not how often either one happens.
