Method

ratings
Simple Rating System: margin = rating_home - rating_away + HFA, solved by least squares with a zero-sum constraint. Reference: Sports-Reference SRS definition.
totals
total = a + b*(rating_home + rating_away), OLS on prior games.
win_prob
P(home win) = Phi(predicted margin / residual SD).
walk_forward
Ratings for week W use only games with week < W, so no game is predicted using information from itself or from a later week.
season_coverage
Only the 2026 season is used, because that is the only season whose game results have been transcribed and verified against official nfl.com pages in this repo.
