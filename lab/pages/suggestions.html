How to use this page

Every suggestion below is PROPOSED. None has been implemented, because the brief asks for a review step where the best path is chosen before anything changes. Each one sets out the problem, two or three options with cost, effort and risk, and a recommendation with the reason for it. Pick a path, record it in the decision column, and only then implement. The executive summary lists the critical and high-priority ones.
