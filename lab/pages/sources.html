What is deliberately not used

The community data mirror is cited on the site and used for nothing that appears in a table. It is the source of the closing lines an earlier session's pipeline consumed, and it carries no official provenance, so no number on this site comes from it.

Every table row on this page links to the official page it came from. A value that could not be confirmed is shown as blank — it is never inferred or filled in. This is a research tool, not betting advice.
