Availability is where the brief asks for the most and where the data is thinnest. The official report tells you a player is Out; it does not tell you who replaced him. That requires a club depth chart, an inactive list and a snap count — and this site has the first for three clubs, the second for one week, and the third for none. Every derived column below says which of those it rests on.

The one structural fact that changes every team model

The injury report and the reserve list are different records, and reading only the first treats a player on injured reserve as available:

Read together, the two official records disagree about who is unavailable; the report alone treats a player on injured reserve as available.

What is deliberately missing here

  • Confirmed starters. Would need official Game Books. Readiness A-06, D-08.
  • Who did not dress. Would need the inactive list for every played fixture. A-04.
  • Who actually played how much. Would need snap counts; the brief forbids inferring depth from jersey order, and without snaps the chart order is all there is. A-05.
  • Whether a designation predicts anything. Would need a play rate per designation. S-13, A-15.
  • A depth chart for 29 clubs. Would need one read per club page. A-16.
