How to read a blank on this site

A blank is never a zero and never an assumption. It means one of three things, and the page says which: not published (the source does not print it), not read (nobody has fetched it yet), or unverified (a value exists but no official page confirms it). Values that are unverified are not shown as numbers at all; they are listed on the Flagged for Review page with what would settle them.
