What this table does not tell you

A row marked met means the artefact exists and the gate is cleared; it does not mean the artefact is good. A row marked partial can still be the most valuable thing on the site. The two rows worth reading first are A-06 (who actually started — blocked on Game Books) and P-02 (the results feed, 137 completed games in the corpus vs 149 implied by the official standings). Almost every other row depends on one of them. Several rows share a single blocker, so the 54 rows are not 54 independent problems: the price policy, the collector run and the Game Book ingest would move more than half of them.
