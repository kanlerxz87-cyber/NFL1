Parlays and prop combos

Parlays are not ranked. Multiplying marginal probabilities is invalid under dependence, so a combo may only be ranked when a directly validated joint probability and the operator's void/push rules are both available. Neither exists in the repository.
Pairs scoredPairs surviving the family-wise thresholdReadingSource
661A correlation_ratio above 1.00 means the two legs landed together more often than independence predicts. Because a standard parlay is priced at the product of the legs, positively correlated legs make the parlay cheaper than it should be -- which is why some pairs show a positive return even though neither leg does on its own. That is a property of the pricing rule, not proof of skill, and it is why every pair is published rather than the best one.parlays.json

What this page will not claim

A positive difference is not an edge. The edge test is correlation C3 above: if the model's disagreement with the market carries no information (interval spanning zero), a large difference is more likely a model error than a market error. It spans zero.
