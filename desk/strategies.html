The competition is ranked on proper scores with uncertainty, not on win rate. On this evidence nothing is promotable: no strategy's profit leaves its own luck band, and the model's probabilities score worse than the closing line. A blank champion is the honest result of 12 tested rules on one partial season, and the page says so before the table.

The leaderboard, with the luck band beside every result

The rightmost columns are the ones that matter. For each strategy the site computes the profit a fair price would have produced on exactly the same bets — mean zero, variance taken from the prices themselves — and the range that profit would fall in 95% of the time. A profit inside that band is indistinguishable from luck. Every row is inside its band.

Pre-registration

31 rules are registered (12 backtest, 19 prospective). A backtest number may be published only when a registration record exists for the rule and the registration commit is an ancestor of the publishing commit. Enforced by tools/check_preregistration.py, which fails the build otherwise. The registration commit is recorded with a hash of the file as committed, so a rule cannot be edited after its results are known.

Open conflict. The s9cb leaderboard above still ranks 12 backtest rows by ROI, which the brief forbids. The page labels them and publishes the sample, but ranking them at all is the defect; it is suggestion DS-03 and readiness row S-04.
