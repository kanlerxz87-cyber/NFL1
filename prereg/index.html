What this page is

The brief requires: “Pre-register every strategy; forbid backtest numbers from ever appearing on the leaderboard” and “Freeze a rule before it sees data … the commit hash is the registration.” This sub-site is the freeze ledger. Historical results live only on the backtest archive, which is explicitly not a leaderboard.

  • Registration kinds. prospective (registration precedes every forecast and result) · prospective-eligible (never evaluated; first evaluation must cite the hash) · retrospective (rules and results already existed together — disclosure only, never a pre-registration claim; flag R26-01 pattern).
  • Every entry carries a two-sided what would change our mind field (upside trigger + downside trigger) — readiness PR-24.
  • Corpus. NFL1-CORPUS-2026-11-15-v1 — 7,104 completed games frozen with a train/validation/holdout split; the 2025–2026 holdout is closed until a recorded decision opens it — readiness PR-09.
  • Controls. a random-side and a favourites-only control are registered for every future board run — readiness PR-17.
