Executive summary

One page for the reader who will not open anything else. What exists, what it proves, what it does not, and the four things only the owner can decide.

Every number below is read from the repository's data files at build time. The source for each is linked in the section where it is used.

1. What this project is

An evidence-first NFL decision site. It publishes its own spread, total and win-probability lines; scores betting strategies against observed closing prices under eight validation gates it declares in advance; tracks official injuries and club depth charts; and refuses to publish a number that cannot be traced to a source. Where a value cannot be confirmed from an official page, it stays blank and is listed as flagged.

2. What was added in this session

  • A personnel-deficiency study — the question the brief asks that the site previously could not answer: do injury and lineup losses carry information the price has not already taken? It is built from 14,478 team-games and tested two ways: whether the measure predicts the result, and whether it predicts the result beyond the closing line.
  • A week-by-week deficiency board — who is ruled out, split into front seven, secondary, quarterback, skill and offensive line, plus which clubs are starting a quarterback who did not start their previous game.
  • A Review Room — this section. Seven decisions, the questions that gate them, ten improvements, and every check this session ran, on separate pages that a non-developer can read.
  • Two defects in this session's own work, found and fixed — a QB-continuity definition that mislabelled a club that had already changed starter, and a clean injury report that was being treated as a missing one. Both are recorded rather than quietly corrected.

3. The headline finding

Personnel information has not beaten the closing line. Of 13 measures tested, 0 survived the correction for 13 tests. The strongest raw result — away ruled-out offensive players — has a raw p of 0.0105 and a corrected p of 0.136. Of the 5 bet rules built on these measures, 0 passed all eight gates and 1 is on watch.

This is the expected result, and it is worth saying why: injury reports are public and are released on a schedule. A market that contains them cannot be beaten by reading them more carefully. The finding is not that injuries do not matter to games — they plainly do — but that they do not matter relative to the price.

What would change that answer is speed rather than knowledge: a timestamped price history would show whether the line moves after each designation, and how fast. That is decision D02, and it is the only route to an edge this analysis can see.

6. The four things only you can decide

  1. Name one authoritative site (D01). Six generations answer the same question differently. Until one is named, every number on this project is one of several.
  2. Buy prices or stop claiming profit (D02). Every ROI figure currently rests on a mirrored closing number, not a price a bettor could take.
  3. Decide what the leaderboard rewards, and whether it can be paid (D03). No official source defines a reward formula, and paying one is a regulated activity.
  4. Accept the negative personnel result, or fund a second attempt (D04). Option B needs defensive player data; option C needs the price feed from D02.

4 of the seven decisions can be taken today without waiting for anything.

7. What you can rely on, and what you cannot

Reliable: the official standings, schedule, injury and depth-chart transcriptions, each with a source link; the repository gates, which recompute published figures independently; the research extract's own outputs, which reproduce byte for byte from the committed inputs.

Not reliable as a claim of profit: any ROI on this site. The prices are a mirrored closing line with no book, limit or timestamp. The records are indicative of model accuracy, not of what a bettor could have collected.

Stale, and disclosed: the committed standings are one game behind the official page as of this session, because the Week 10 Thursday game had finished. The difference is published club by club on the Verification page rather than silently corrected.
