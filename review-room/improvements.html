What is deliberately not suggested

More strategy rules. The repository already scores seventeen of them and none passes every gate. Adding an eighteenth rule to a family that is already corrected for multiplicity does not produce a winner; it produces a smaller p-value threshold. The work that would change the answer is prices and speed, not more rules.
