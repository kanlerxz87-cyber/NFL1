The short version, in five sentences

  1. The site already answers the factual questions well: 32 clubs, the week-10 injury report, the depth charts, this week's own lines. That part is verified and publishes an official link on every row.
  2. It does not yet answer the money questions, because it has no real prices. Nothing here can tell you whether any strategy made a profit a bettor could have collected.
  3. This session added the piece the brief asked for and the site did not have: a test of whether personnel losses — backup quarterbacks, ruled-out defenders, lost offensive production — carry information the closing line has not already priced. Across 13 measures on up to 7,087 games, 0 adds information beyond the price.
  4. That is a real result, and it points the same way the market does: injuries are public, so they are priced. The honest next step is not another injury rule; it is a price feed that can measure the market's reaction.
  5. So the decision queue has two hard doors: buy prices (D02), and name one authoritative site (D01). Almost every other item waits behind one of them.

Where the evidence is not on your side

Injuries have not beaten the closing line. This is the finding that matters most from this session, so it is stated plainly and linked rather than softened. Of 13 personnel measures tested against up to 7,087 played games, 0 adds statistically significant information beyond the closing spread once the correction for testing 13 measures is applied. The strongest raw result, away ruled-out offensive players, has a raw p of 0.0105 and a corrected p of 0.136.

The full table, every coefficient and every confidence interval is on the Signals & evidence page. The five bet rules built on those measures are on the same page, with their gates: 0 passed all eight, 1 are on watch.

What this site is not

  • Not advice. No row here says to place a bet, and none can until D02 is settled.
  • Not a prediction service. The published own lines are a model output, labelled as one; where an official value exists, the official value is what is shown.
  • Not complete. Every gap is on the Questions page, every irregularity is on Flagged for review, and anything that could not be confirmed from an official source is blank rather than filled in.
