Fixture list and kickoff times: official Week 10 schedule. Game status and practice status: official injury report.

Read this before the table

The official report publishes who is Out, Doubtful or Questionable. It does not publish who replaced whom. The replacement a club intends is on its own depth chart, and the replacement who actually played is only knowable after snap counts. This page therefore reports what is published and nothing else: it never infers a starter from a depth chart, and it never assumes a ruled-out player was replaced by a specific backup.

Where a club has no ruled-out player, the value is a real zero and is shown as 0. Where a club has no row in the report at all, the cell is blank and says so.

Official page vs the mirrored feed

Two official-data paths exist in this repository and they do not always agree. The direct nfl.com scrape reads the league page itself; the mirrored feed is a third-party copy of the same report. Comparing them is a check on the feed, and a club where they disagree is a club whose row was stale in one of them.

What this table does not tell you

  • Whether it matters. The Signals page tests exactly that, and the answer this session is that these counts do not move the result beyond the closing line.
  • Who plays. Final inactives are published closer to kickoff and a Questionable player often plays.
  • How much a backup is worse. That needs the backup's own performance history, which is on the main site's injuries and depth page.
