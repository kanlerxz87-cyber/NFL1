Why this is the honest answer

An earlier version of this analysis reported four PASSes with hit rates above 90%. That was a sign-convention bug: it treated the closing line as points given to the home club instead of the home club's expected margin. After the fix every rule fails, and the sign check is now a unit test. The gates did their job — which is the only reason to trust the zero.
