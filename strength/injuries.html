Why there is no 2nd/3rd/4th-string deficiency score here

The brief asks for a system that knows when a backup is replacing a starter. Three things are missing, and each one would be a guess if filled in.

  1. Game-day inactives. The weekly report lists who is hurt; it does not say who is dressing.
  2. A dated depth-chart snapshot. Club sites publish depth charts, but not with a reliable timestamp that can be joined to a specific kick-off, and nfl.com publishes no depth chart.
  3. Snap counts to measure the replacement. Without them, "backup played" cannot be turned into "backup played worse".

So the honest output is what is on this page: the official status of every listed player, a link for each row, and the three data additions that would let a deficiency score be measured rather than assumed (S-SOS-05, Q-SOS-09).
