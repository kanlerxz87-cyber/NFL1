A parlay of two positively correlated props is worth less than the product of the two prices; a parlay of negatively correlated props is worth more. Without a price neither can be converted into a decision, so the track stays empty and the leaderboard carries no prop entries.

What would unblock it

  1. A licensed odds feed with per-book prop prices and timestamps (see S-SOS-04).
  2. Inactive lists per game, because a prop depends on who is actually dressed.
  3. A pre-registered combo rule set, so the evaluation is not chosen after the fact.
