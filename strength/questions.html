Q-SOS-01 — Does strength of schedule, measured walk-forward, add information beyond the closing line? ANSWERED

Own ratings correlate r=0.466 with the closing spread over 7,103 games, but the own line is less accurate than the market out of sample: spread MAE 10.714 vs 9.63 in the untouched holdout seasons, and every one of the 11 schedule-based rules fails the gate set (0 of 11 PASS). The honest reading is that schedule information is already inside the price.

How to discover it: computed in analysis/strength.py; re-runnable · What it blocks: any decision to trade schedule-based angles

Q-SOS-03 — Whose record overstates them, and whose understates them? ANSWERED

Luck = actual wins minus Pythagorean expectation (exponent 2.37). Most lucky: DEN +2.17; BUF +1.58; CLE +1.53; DET +1.25; SF +1.20. Most unlucky: TB -2.17; LAC -1.57; TEN -1.54; NYJ -1.44; HOU -1.40. Caveat: the rules that trade on this (X05, X06) both fail the gates, so the signal is a description, not an edge.

How to discover it: Pythagorean expectation computed from official scored/allowed points · What it blocks: nothing; published as a description

Q-SOS-08 — Are the 2026 closing lines official NFL numbers? ANSWERED

Q-SOS-11 — Is the gate set strong enough to stop a lucky strategy? ANSWERED

During this session the gates rejected a first implementation whose sign convention made the market line a points-given rather than an expected margin: it reported 94% cover rates and four false PASSes. After the fix every rule fails, and the same sign check now lives in the unit tests. The lesson is that a plausible-looking edge is the default failure mode, and the gates must be able to say no.

How to discover it: unit tests in tests/test_strength.py plus manual inspection of the bet log · What it blocks: nothing

Q-SOS-12 — What would we need to get a real, bettable edge? BLOCKED

At minimum: (1) a licensed historical odds feed with per-book, timestamped prices and line movement; (2) injury/inactive data as it stood at kick-off, not today's; (3) a pre-registered hypothesis set fixed before peeking; (4) enough seasons that a 2% edge is distinguishable from zero at the tested sample size. Without (1) and (2) any edge claim is untestable.

How to discover it: gap analysis of the sources in data/s9cb/provenance.json · What it blocks: any decision to place or recommend a wager
