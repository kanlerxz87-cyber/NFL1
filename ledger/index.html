NFL Origination Ledger

A pre-registered test of whether an own-made NFL number beats the closing market: 12 rules, 10 gates each, on 5,336 decided games. The answer, stated up front, is no — and the page below shows the evidence, the exact gates that failed, and what would have to change.

The one-paragraph answer. The pre-registered own-line model is a worse forecaster than the closing line on every measure computed here: its Brier score is 0.2255 against the de-vigged close's 0.2109 (a skill score of -0.0694, interval excluding zero), and it is the only one of the twelve rules whose in-sample ROI is materially positive (C84-S4, 2.32%), which fails multiple testing and fails the untouched 2024-2026 holdout. Adding the availability features to the fit changes mean absolute error by 0.0007 points with an interval containing zero, so the injury signal is not doing work. No rule is promoted and nothing on this site is a bet recommendation.
