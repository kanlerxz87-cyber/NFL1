Own lines & the price store

The brief's first two requirements are a price-snapshot store and a results feed, then: publish the model's fair line, the market line and the difference as separate columns. This page does that, and states plainly which of the three columns can be verified.

The price-snapshot store cannot be built from the committed data, and this page does not pretend otherwise. The feed carries ONE number per market per game. There is no opening number and no fetch timestamp, so an open-to-close study is impossible. What exists is a single closing snapshot, labelled as unofficial. Requirement PR-01 is therefore Blocked, not Done, and the requirements page says what would clear it.
