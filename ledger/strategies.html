Why no parlay or prop combo is ranked

Multiplying marginal probabilities is invalid under dependence, and this sample shows the dependence is not zero: the correlation between a game's margin and its total points is 0.0201 on 5,336 games. Two legs inside one game therefore cannot be priced by multiplication, and no observed prop price exists in any committed source, so a prop combo has neither a price nor a joint probability. Both are required before a combo may be ranked; neither exists. Combos are not listed, not scored and not recommended.
