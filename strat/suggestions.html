How a suggestion becomes a change

  1. Every suggestion names the problem, the proposal, its cost, its risk and the decision it feeds.
  2. Nothing is implemented before the owner records a choice in the decisions register.
  3. A suggestion that needs information nobody publishes is converted into a question, not built.
  4. The owner can accept, reject or defer; the record of the choice is the gate.

Nothing on this page has been implemented on the strength of the suggestion alone. The two that are already built (the competition overview and the two-totals page) are marked as such in the cost column, because they are this session's own deliverables and hiding that would defeat the purpose of the register.
