The gate

A live strategy returns to paper when any of five rules below fires. The thresholds were fixed before any live bet existed, so a later change is a diff in this repository rather than an adjustment made after seeing a result.

RuleNameFires whenMinimum sampleMeasured onWhy this thresholdFrozen inOfficial record it reads
D1skill interval collapses90% interval on Brier skill vs the price has a lower bound < 050live (post-registration, settled) bets50 settled bets is the smallest sample at which the interval has any power at realistic edges.data/strat/demotion.json ↗official price record ↗
D2stop-lossrealised return < -10%50live (post-registration, settled) betsAbout four times the best backtest ROI in the run, so a live -10% is not the same scale of noise.data/strat/demotion.json ↗official price record ↗
D3hit rate below break-evenhit rate < price-implied break-even - 2 standard errors100live (post-registration, settled) bets100 bets is the smallest sample at which a 2-SE gap can resolve a 2-point edge.data/strat/demotion.json ↗official price record ↗
D4losing streak12 consecutive settled losses12live (post-registration, settled) betsRoughly the 1-in-500 point of a fair sequence at the prices these rules take; fires before D2 can.data/strat/demotion.json ↗official price record ↗
D5definition driftthe rule text or inputs differ from the registration commit0the registration recordA rule whose text changed after its result was seen is a new rule and must start at paper.data/strat/demotion.json ↗official price record ↗

The rule text and thresholds are frozen in the artifact linked on each row; a change is a diff in this repository, and the official price record the gate reads is the NFL schedule pages linked on the leaderboard.

Re-promotion

a fresh prospective sample, 200 settled bets, clearing D1-D3 in the opposite direction. The same sample that demoted a strategy cannot also promote it; that is the definition of chasing.
