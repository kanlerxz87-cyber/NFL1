Why this table is not ordered by win rate

Win rate is not a proper scoring rule. A forecast that is right 60% of the time while being badly calibrated is worse than one that is right 52% of the time with honest probabilities, and ROI on a few hundred bets cannot separate the two. The first column here is benchmark-relative Brier skill, and the published figure is the lower bound of a game-cluster bootstrap interval, not the point estimate.

What is deliberately absent

Parlay, same-game-parlay and player-prop divisions are blank: multiplying marginal probabilities is invalid under dependence and no prop price exists in this repository. A blank here is a finding about the data, not a zero.
