The one-paragraph verdict

Nothing in this repository has demonstrated skill against the closing line. The model's side probabilities score -0.0413 on the Brier skill scale (positive is better), its totals -0.0346, and its moneylines -0.0714 against the de-vigged market. All three intervals sit below zero. The closing line, by contrast, beats a coin on the moneyline by +0.1564 with an interval above zero — which is a statement about the market, not about us.

This is the result the gates were built to be able to produce. A site that could only report wins would not be measuring anything.
