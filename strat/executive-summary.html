Strategy competition

Executive Summary

What was built, what the evidence says, what is still unknown, and what the owner must decide.

Built 2026-11-15T13:03:39+00:00 UTC · every table row links to its source · a blank cell means the value is not confirmed

What was built

  • A pre-registration registry — 31 rules with frozen text, inputs, sample windows, gates and the registering commit; 12 labelled backtest (selection-exposed) and 19 prospective (no result yet). A build gate forbids a published result without a preceding registration.
  • A proper-score leaderboard — benchmark-relative Brier skill per market with a game-cluster bootstrap lower bound, a multiplicity charge over both the market family and the rule family, and five control baselines.
  • A demotion gate — five pre-registered rules with thresholds fixed before any live bet, plus a pre-registered re-promotion condition.
  • Two-sided falsifiers on 12 published claims, each with a confirming and an abandoning threshold.
  • Two published totals with a gap column and a walk-forward verdict.
  • 12 blocking questions, 12 decisions that need only a choice, and 12 suggestions with costs and risks, in one review-before-implementation flow.

The best point estimate in the run is S07 Fade the road favourite of 7+'s Brier skill versus its own price, +0.0104 on 394 decided bets, with an ROI interval that includes zero — a value far inside the noise band.

What is still unknown, and what would settle it

  1. Whether the historical extract matches the official record — Q03; a Game Book reconciliation of 50 random games per season.
  2. Whether the official terms permit the publication — Q01; the highest-priority decision on the board.
  3. Whether an approved price source exists — Q02; without it no ROI claim can ever be published.
  4. Whether availability surprise carries information — Q12 and W09; needs official inactive lists and Game Books.
  5. Whether the total model adds anything over a no-model baseline — W05; currently undecided (-0.0136, interval -0.2288 to +0.2188).

What the owner must decide

The 12 decisions are on the questions page. Three matter more than the rest:

  1. D03 — settle the official-data terms question. Until it is settled, every additional data layer raises the cost of the eventual answer.
  2. D02 — pre-register a promotion rule before any live bet. The competition currently has an exit and no entrance.
  3. D12 — pre-register the effective number of independent tests for the rule family, so the multiplicity charge is neither too harsh nor too lenient.

Honest bottom line

This session's contribution is not a winning strategy. It is the machinery that would let a winning strategy be recognised — and the published result that, on the evidence available, there is not one. That is a finding, and it is the one the gates were designed to be able to produce.

Nothing on this site is a recommendation to bet. The demotion gate is armed; nothing is live; 31 strategies sit on paper, which is where the evidence puts them.
