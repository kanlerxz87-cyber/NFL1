Luck and sensitivity

How good must a record be before luck stops being the likely explanation?

A bet at -110 breaks even at 52.38% wins. A strategy with no edge at all will sometimes beat that by chance, and the more strategies are tried, the better the luckiest one looks. This page puts numbers on it.
