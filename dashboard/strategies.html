Findings (model output, unverified)

  • S11 is labelled WATCH with 6 of 8 gates. It fails significance (G5) and stability (G6, 13 of 22 seasons positive). The status rule counts gates, so WATCH does not mean significance (RV-08).
  • S04 and S06 are the same bet. They share 207 games and have correlation 1.0 on that overlap. Nine of twelve strategies show |corr| = 1.0 on the diversification gate (RV-09).
  • Benjamini-Hochberg q-values are identical (0.9889). This reproduces from the published p-values, so it is not a bug. It means no strategy is distinguishable from chance after correction (RV-10).
  • Parlays and player-prop combos. No prices are published for them, so no performance value is shown (flag UNV-PERF).
  • Rewards. Any monetary reward needs legal review before use (flag F05, from the earlier flags list).
