What the depth-chart join gives us, and what it does not

QuestionAnswerSource
Can we tell whether an absent player is a listed starter?Yes, for the current season. Weekly depth charts carry a position rank where 1 is the listed starter, and an earlier session joined them to the injury report.nfl.com ↗
Can we tell who replaces him?Yes, for the current season: the next rank at that position is on the same chart.nfl.com ↗
Can we tell whether the replacement is any good?Not yet. Historical depth charts are not in the repository, so no backup-quality rule can be back-tested. Flagged as U06.nfl.com ↗
Are the practice-only weights official?No. Out = 1.0, Doubtful = 0.7, Questionable = 0.25, with practice-only weights of 0.5 for did-not-participate and 0.2 for limited, are a modelling choice, not a league rule.operations.nfl.com ↗
