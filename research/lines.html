How the line is made

StepChoiceWhy
RatingsRidge regression on team attack and defence terms, fitted separately for margin and totalSmall enough to read, and it separates 'scores a lot' from 'concedes little'.
Walk-forwardRefitted for every (season, week) block using only games that finished earlierA line for week 10 has never seen week 10 or anything after it.
Recency weightingExponential, half-life chosen from [8.0, 16.0, 30.0, 60.0, 120.0] weeksTeams change; a 2019 game should count for less than a 2026 one.
Hyper-parameter choiceSelected on the newest 15% of the training games onlyThe half-life is never tuned on the games being scored.
Ridge penalty12.0Shrinks extreme small-sample team ratings toward the mean.

The walk-forward guard is tested, not asserted. The 2025 week-12 block was re-fitted with future data added: the two prediction sets are different, so the guard is real. Training rows: 6,859 walk-forward against 7,090 with the future included.
