Executive summary

The repository began as a six-byte README. It now runs an automated data pipeline, originates its own NFL lines from opponent-adjusted play-by-play, scores strategies against the closing line under eight validation gates, tracks starter availability against the weekly depth chart, and publishes every table with a source link and every gap as a flag. Nothing in this build required manual data entry; the only hand-entered values are the 128 verification rows transcribed from nfl.com to prove the feed matches the league's own pages.

What was verified

  • 119 of 128 verification rows matched the source they were checked against (2026 standings (W-L-T, points for/against); Scraped official injury report vs hand-transcribed sample; Computed standings vs companion transcription (data/standings.csv); Scraped official report vs companion injury CSV (data/injuries.csv)).
  • 12 source files pinned with download URL, byte count, sha256 and fetch time.
  • The current week's official injury report is scraped directly from nfl.com: 295 rows across the clubs that had submitted a report, matched to the weekly depth chart. 2 clubs had no report at scrape time (BAL, LAC).
  • The mirrored feed is incomplete in-season: 276 of 295 official week 10 rows were present in it, which is exactly why the official page is scraped directly.
  • The closing-line sign convention was proved from data: correlation 0.439 with the home margin over 5591 games.
  • nflverse file licence verified as CC-BY-4.0 (attribution shown in the footer).
  • A second, independent reading of the official pages on the same day: 841 comparisons of the published tables against two verbatim transcriptions of the official league standings page and the official Week 10 schedule, 0 of them failing. 64 irregularities were found on the official pages themselves and are published on the Official-Source Audit page — they are quoted, not corrected.

What the numbers say

  • Our lines are not yet better than the market. Out-of-sample spread MAE 9.98 points versus 9.48 for the closing line, and the paired difference interval excludes zero — the market is significantly closer. The totals model is similarly behind (11.31 vs 10.60).
  • The model's incremental information is measurable, not assumed. Blend test: market weight 1.336, model weight -0.328, test MAE 9.54 vs 9.48 market-only over 136 games.
  • No strategy earns promotion. 12 rules were tested; 0 passed all eight gates. The top score belongs to S11 Under in divisional games with total 47+ with ROI 4.2% on 548 bets and a lower 95% bound of -3.9%.
  • Same-game legs are only weakly linked. phi(home covers, over hits) = 0.009 across 5591 games, so naive parlay multiplication is roughly right for spread/total combos but must never be used for player-prop combos without their own correlation.
  • Availability is concentrated. The injury table names every unavailable starter, the next man at that slot, his experience and his 2026 snaps, so a reviewer can see which clubs are leaning on 2nd and 3rd string players.

What remains unresolved

  • No official or book-level prices: edge, CLV and prop value are unmeasurable (F01, F02).
  • Depth chart rank is not participation; the injury effect is not yet quantified (F03).
  • Rewards with real value need legal review (F04).
  • Model history covers 2024-2026 only, so confidence intervals are wide (F06).
  • Weather, travel and inactives are missing, so those signals are untested (F09, F10).
  • The official standings page does not reproduce its own split columns between two readings and contradicts the clubs' games played in 23 printed rows, so only W-L-T, PCT, PF, PA and NET are published as verified facts (F15, F16, F17).

Decisions requested

  1. Approve the hybrid line as the published default, with the pure model kept visible.
  2. Fund a licensed odds feed, or accept that the site states model-only findings.
  3. Keep the rewards competition on paper points until counsel signs off.
  4. Choose the risk policy: flat 1 unit with a -25 unit review trigger.
  5. Approve ingesting game-day inactives and participation data next.

How to read the site

  • My Lines — own spread, total, win probability, error bands, ratings, and the accuracy table.
  • Strategy Lab — leaderboard, gate matrix, correlation, parlay maths, rewards rules.
  • Injuries & Depth — starters out, replacements, attrition index.
  • Props — projections with intervals and deliberately blank market columns.
  • Official Audit — every published number compared with the official pages, line by line.
  • Questions — the decision gate: known, unknown, how to find out.
  • Suggestions — twelve ranked changes and five decision records.
  • Evidence — provenance and line-by-line verification.
  • Flagged for Review — everything unverified or blocked.

Constraints this build respects

  • No value is inferred, interpolated or invented. Blanks are labelled.
  • Every strategy rule is fixed before evaluation; the count tested is published so the multiple-testing correction can be checked.
  • Ratings never use information from the game being predicted.
  • Every page states what it cannot do.
