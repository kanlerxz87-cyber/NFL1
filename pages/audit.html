What this page is

Session 4c043747 read the official NFL league standings page twice on 2026-11-12 (about 22:05Z and 22:25Z) and transcribed all 32 clubs, every printed column, and the official Week 10 schedule with the records the schedule page prints beside each club. The harness tools/audit_official.py then compared every standings table published in this repository with that transcription and published the Week 10 model slate against the official slate.

The two retrievals are stored verbatim in data/audit/4c043747/ so any line can be re-checked by hand: transcription files.
