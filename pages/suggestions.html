Questions that must be answered (from 690e397b)

Product & audience

  • Research dashboard, public predictions, or private decision tool?
  • Which leagues, seasons, markets, books, and jurisdictions are in scope?
  • Is this strictly informational/paper testing, or is any wagering/reward contemplated?

Data & provenance

  • Which sources are permitted, licensed, stable, and historically complete?
  • What are update latency, archival rights, correction behavior, and costs?
  • Which official source can verify depth-chart order and replacement roles with dates?

Evaluation

  • What precisely counts as a bet, price snapshot, close, push, void, and settled result?
  • Which benchmark, metrics, minimum sample, confidence level, and promotion threshold?
  • How will overlapping parlays, correlated markets, and repeated experiments be controlled?

Governance & safety

  • Who reviews strategy submissions, disputes, cheating, and data errors?
  • What exposure, drawdown, access, responsible-use and jurisdiction controls are required?
  • What are prize eligibility, funding, tax, terms, and independent verification rules?
