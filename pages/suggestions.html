How to choose a path without guessing

Each suggestion names the problem, the change, the evidence that would prove it worked, and what it blocks. Nothing here is implemented silently: pick a decision record, pilot one change, and compare the named metric before adopting it.

  1. 1. Read the flag
    Every item on the review page names the missing evidence, the source that would settle it and what it blocks.
  2. 2. Pick a decision record
    D01-D05 cover the choices that gate the most work. Record the choice, the date and the reason in the coordination board.
  3. 3. Pilot one suggestion
    Implement a single suggestion behind a labelled section; do not change several at once.
  4. 4. Measure against the criteria
    Re-run the pipeline; compare the published metric named in the suggestion.
  5. 5. Adopt, revise or revert
    If the metric does not improve out of sample, revert and log it as a negative result.

S01 Make the closing line the baseline for every published number

Implemented

Problem: Own lines are currently compared to nothing, so a number cannot be judged.

Change: Show model line, market line and the difference side by side on every game, with the model's error band.

Impact
High - turns opinions into measurable deltas.
Effort
Done in this build
Risk
Low
Depends on
Closing lines (present)
Evidence it is right
MAE table and calibration rows on the Lines page.
Recommendation
Keep. This is the backbone of every later decision.

S02 Blend the model with the market instead of pretending to beat it

Implemented - needs sign-off as default

Problem: The EPA model is less accurate thanthe closing line on its own.

Change: Publish a hybrid line with a weight fitted on one season and tested on the next; show the weight.

Impact
High - prevents using a weaker model as if it were truth.
Effort
Done in this build
Risk
Low if the weight is re-fitted each season.
Depends on
Walk-forward ratings, market lines
Evidence it is right
Blend test block on the Lines page (weight on the model, test MAE).
Recommendation
Adopt as the default published line; keep the pure model visible for transparency.

S03 Buy a licensed odds feed with timestamps and limits

Proposed - decision required

Problem: There is no official league odds source and no open/close history.

Change: Integrate one licensed feed (book, price, limit, timestamp), store append-only, and price every strategy at the actual quoted number.

Impact
Very high - unlocks CLV, prop pricing, parlay pricing and honest ROI.
Effort
Medium (contract + integration)
Risk
Cost; vendor reliability; terms of use
Depends on
Budget approval
Evidence it is right
Once live: per-bet price provenance and CLV distribution.
Recommendation
Highest-priority purchase. Until then no edge claim is valid.

S04 Preregister the rulebook before each season

Proposed

Problem: Rules can be added after seeing results, which manufactures significance.

Change: Freeze a dated rulebook in git; score only those rules; publish how many were tested.

Impact
High - makes gate G2 real instead of aspirational.
Effort
Low
Risk
Less flexibility
Depends on
Nothing
Evidence it is right

Adopt immediately for 2027.

S05 Model who actually plays, not who is listed first

Proposed - queued

Problem: Depth chart rank is not participation; the injury edge is unmeasured.

Change: Use participation and snap-count data to compute replacement snap share and re-test injury strategies.

Impact
High - the injury requirement in the brief depends on it.
Effort
Medium
Risk
Participation data lags; coverage per season varies
Depends on
nflverse participation files (available), inactives page
Evidence it is right
Backtest of injury rules with actual snap share as the treatment variable.
Recommendation
Do next, before any injury-driven line adjustment is published as an edge.

S06 Keep the rewards competition on paper points with anti-overfitting rules

Proposed

Problem: Any real-value prize invites overfitting and, in some jurisdictions, regulation.

Change: Score by lower confidence bound plus stability and gate count; cap entries; require out-of-sample seasons.

Impact
Medium - keeps the leaderboard honest.
Effort
Low
Risk
Legal review needed before real value
Depends on
Q11 legal review
Evidence it is right
Published scoring formula and the gate table.
Recommendation
Adopt paper points now; revisit real rewards after counsel.

S09 Backfill play-by-play to 1999 to buy statistical power

Proposed - queued

Problem: 2024-2026 gives roughly 400 rated games; significance is hard to reach.

Change: Ingest 1999-2023 play-by-play and rebuild walk-forward ratings.

Impact
Medium - narrows confidence intervals on the model comparison.
Effort
Low-medium (more data, same code)
Risk
Rule changes make old seasons less comparable
Depends on
Pinned historical files
Evidence it is right
Blend weight and MAE stability by decade.
Recommendation
Adopt before drawing conclusions about the model.
