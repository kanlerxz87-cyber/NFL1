Suggestions

These are decision options for a reviewer to compare. They do not silently authorize data collection, paid services, real-money betting, or prizes. Sequence choices by evidence and risk.

PrioritySuggestionDecision / tradeoffProposed acceptance criterionDependency
1 · FoundationAgree allowed data sources, licensing, retention, and update cadence; build a source registry with retrieval timestamps and record-level citations.Official league sources are authoritative for league records but may not supply odds or every required historical feature; a licensed provider may be needed.Every populated field has source URL, retrieved-at time, entity/time scope, and a documented correction policy.Data rights/legal review; feeds not selected
2 · Market integrityChoose target markets and benchmark odds capture policy, including market close definition and book/provider.Broad market coverage increases complexity and multiple-testing risk; one market is easier to audit.Frozen, reproducible quotes are available for each eligible prediction and settlement.Price feed/provider and historical availability
3 · AvailabilityCreate injury/replacement tracker with report chronology; track offense and defense separately, backup order and actual role evidence.Depth chart designation alone may not capture actual usage; role/participation must be timestamped and sourced.For each relevant absence, preserve official report and dated roster/role evidence; unknown depth remains blank.Official reports; authoritative depth/role source still to identify

Questions that must be answered

Product & audience

  • Research dashboard, public predictions, or private decision tool?
  • Which leagues, seasons, markets, books, and jurisdictions are in scope?
  • Is this strictly informational/paper testing, or is any wagering/reward contemplated?

Data & provenance

  • Which sources are permitted, licensed, stable, and historically complete?
  • What are update latency, archival rights, correction behavior, and costs?
  • Which official source can verify depth-chart order and replacement roles with dates?

Evaluation

  • What precisely counts as a bet, price snapshot, close, push, void, and settled result?
  • Which benchmark, metrics, minimum sample, confidence level, and promotion threshold?
  • How will overlapping parlays, correlated markets, and repeated experiments be controlled?

Governance & safety

  • Who reviews strategy submissions, disputes, cheating, and data errors?
  • What exposure, drawdown, access, responsible-use and jurisdiction controls are required?
  • What are prize eligibility, funding, tax, terms, and independent verification rules?
