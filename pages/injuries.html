What this measures

The brief asks for defence and offence deficiencies when 2nd, 3rd or 4th string players replace starters. The feed gives two things reliably: the official game availability status and the weekly depth chart. Joining them identifies which listed starters are unavailable and who is next at that exact position slot, then measures the replacement's experience and 2026 snaps. What it cannot yet do is prove who took the first snap — see the flags.

Depth chart mirrored weekly (pos_rank 1 = listed starter). sum over unavailable starters of position_weight x status_weight; game weights Out=1.0, Doubtful=0.7, Questionable=0.25.
