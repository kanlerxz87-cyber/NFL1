Options for review (not implemented)

Each option closes part of the depth-order gap. None is applied; choose one, or none, before any change.

  1. Keep depth order blank (current). Shows official status and official same-group backups only. Trade-off: no 2nd / 3rd / 4th-string ranking.
  2. Parse club-published depth charts from each club's own site (the nfl.com roster pages link to them, e.g. detroitlions.com/team/depth-chart). Trade-off: 32 different page layouts; each club page must be re-read and checked before use. This is the most direct official route.
  3. Use the nflverse depth-chart mirror already in the pipeline. Trade-off: not an official NFL source, so under the site rules its values must stay blank or be labelled unverified.
  4. Add snap counts (who actually played) to show backups used in practice. Trade-off: needs a source that is itself verified; not yet identified.

Method and limits

  • Designations: official nfl.com game status Out, Doubtful or Questionable (week 10 report).
  • Match: the injury row is matched to the official roster row of the same club by full name; no match or more than one match is flagged and no backups are listed.
  • Backups: other players on the same club's official roster with roster status ACT and the same position group (mapping in tools/build_backup_register.py). Listed alphabetically, not ranked.
  • Depth order (2nd / 3rd / 4th string) is not published by nfl.com, so it is left blank.
  • Generic 'DB' roster players cannot be assigned to CB or S, so they are listed but not counted.
  • Roster pages show the roster at the time they were fetched; they are not a Week 10 snapshot.

Sources: nfl.com/injuries · nfl.com team directory · per-club rosters at nfl.com/teams/<club>/roster (linked in every row).
