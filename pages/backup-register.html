Method and limits

  • Designations: official nfl.com game status Out, Doubtful or Questionable (week 10 report).
  • Match: the injury row is matched to the official roster row of the same club by full name; no match or more than one match is flagged and no backups are listed.
  • Backups: other players on the same club's official roster with roster status ACT and the same position group (mapping in tools/build_backup_register.py). Listed alphabetically, not ranked.
  • Depth order (2nd / 3rd / 4th string) is not published by nfl.com, so it is left blank.
  • Generic 'DB' roster players cannot be assigned to CB or S, so they are listed but not counted.
  • Roster pages show the roster at the time they were fetched; they are not a Week 10 snapshot.

Sources: nfl.com/injuries · nfl.com team directory · per-club rosters at nfl.com/teams/<club>/roster (linked in every row).
