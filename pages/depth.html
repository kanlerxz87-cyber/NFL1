How the ladder is built, and what it cannot see

Where the ladder comes from

Official club depth charts, https://www.<club>/team/depth-chart, transcribed 2026-11-12. nfl.com publishes no depth chart: https://www.nfl.com/teams/kansas-city-chiefs/depth-chart returns 404 (re-checked 2026-11-13).

Who counts as unavailable

Projected starter = the lowest-ranked listed player at that slot who is not Out or Doubtful on the official report. Questionable counts as available but at risk and is shown, never upgraded or downgraded.

What is left blank

Clubs with no published report this week (on bye, or the club has filed nothing) are labelled 'no report published'. Their ladders are published but no starter is marked unavailable, because the absence of a report is not evidence of health.

  • Club depth charts are a club publication, not a league filing; several are labelled unofficial by the club itself and the label is carried through.
  • The chart is a weekly snapshot. Practice-squad elevations, trades and late scratches are not modelled.
  • Game status is a designation, not proof of who plays.
  • HOU publishes no depth chart; its rows are blank by design.
