How to use this page

These are the questions that must be answered before a decision is reasonable. Each row states what we already have, what is missing, how it could be discovered, and what it blocks. A question marked Blocked cannot be solved with the sources currently reachable from the build environment, so it needs a purchase, a permission or a human decision.

Q01Which price, at which book, at what timestamp, is being evaluated?
A bet is only as good as its price. ROI cannot be reconstructed from a single closing number.		Consensus closing spread/total/moneyline per game (mirrored, not league-published).Book-level quotes, bet limits, open/close snapshots, capture timestamps.Licensed odds API with 15-minute snapshots stored as append-only history; log book, limit, timestamp.Any claim of edge, CLV, or strategy reward ranking.Open - decision required
Q02Does the model beat the closing line, or only beat the opening line?
Beating the close is the accepted evidence of skill; beating a stale open can be luck or a soft book.		Closing lines for 2005-2026; own lines generated walk-forward.Opening lines and full line history.Snapshot odds at T-7d, T-24h and kickoff; recompute the edge at each point.Strategy gate G5 (significance) and any skill claim.Open
Q03Who actually starts, and did a backup play meaningful snaps?
Depth charts are snapshots, not participation. The injury edge depends on who really plays.		Official game status (Out/Doubtful/Questionable), weekly depth charts, snap counts by player.Game-day inactives list, first-drive participation, snap share of the replacement.Ingest the official inactives page and nflverse participation data; compute replacement snap share per game.Quantifying the injury edge (Q08) and any 'backup QB' strategy.Partially answered (snap counts modelled, inactives missing)
Q04Are player props priced at all, and with what correlation for combos?
Without a market price there is no edge to measure on props or same-game parlays.		Own prop projections with intervals; same-game leg correlation measured from results.Prop prices, alternate lines, sportsbook same-game correlation pricing.Licensed prop feed; or restrict to model-only publication until a feed exists.Prop and combo strategies (currently model-only).Blocked - no source
Q05How large a sample is needed before a 3% ROI is believable?
At -110 the per-bet variance is large; most 'winning' strategies are noise at small n.		Per-bet results and bootstrap CIs for every strategy; power arithmetic on this page.Nothing statistical - this is a design decision, not data.Require n >= 100 and a positive lower bound of the 95% ROI interval before ranking.Reward eligibility and any promotion of a strategy.Answered (gate G3)
Q06How many strategies can be tested before results are just multiple testing?
Testing 20 rules at p<0.05 yields one 'winner' by chance.		Benjamini-Hochberg q-values across every rule tested; the count is published.A frozen public list of rules before the season starts (preregistration).Publish a signed rulebook per season; timestamp in git; evaluate only those rules.Gate G2 in future seasons.Partially answered (BH applied; preregistration not yet enforced)
Q07Which injury source is authoritative at kickoff?
Practice reports are predictive, game status is official, inactives are final. They disagree.		Official weekly report with practice and game status.The 90-minute inactive list, and any late scratches.Fetch the inactives page ~90 minutes before each kickoff and store with timestamp.Any injury-driven line adjustment.Open
Q08How many points is a missing starter worth, by position?
The attrition index is a weighting assumption; it is not yet calibrated to results.		Position weights and starter-level availability counts.Estimated point value per position from historical outcomes.Regress margin on availability dummies across 2024-2026 with team fixed effects; validate out of season.Any claim that injuries explain a line move.Open - flagged
Q09Do injuries already move the line, making the signal useless?
If the market prices the backup QB immediately, betting the backup is not an edge.		Closing lines and injury reports for 2025-2026.Opening lines, so the move attributable to the injury cannot be isolated.Compare open vs close for games with a QB ruled out; requires Q02 data.The backup-QB strategy verdict.Blocked by Q02
Q10What bankroll, unit size and drawdown can the user accept?
Even a +EV strategy can lose 25 units; sizing decides survival.		Max drawdown per strategy in units under flat 1-unit staking.Bankroll, risk tolerance, Kelly fraction, stop-loss policy.User decision; then simulate fractional Kelly and ruin probability.Any staking recommendation.Open - user decision
Q11Is a rewards competition lawful where the user is located?
Prize competitions tied to betting performance can be regulated gambling.		A paper-points leaderboard design with zero cash value.Legal review for the relevant jurisdiction; terms of service.Counsel review before any reward with monetary value; keep points non-redeemable until then.Launching rewards beyond paper points.Blocked - legal review
Q12Is the mirrored data licensed for republication?
The site republishes derived tables.		nflverse-data licence verified as CC-BY-4.0 (attribution required). NFL club marks remain the NFL's.Review of NFL.com terms for the hand-transcribed verification rows.Attribute nflverse on every page; keep hand transcription limited to verification samples.Wider redistribution of raw feeds.Answered for nflverse, open for nfl.com rows
Q13Does the model add anything beyond the closing line?
If a blend test gives the model zero weight, the whole line-generation effort is decoration.		Walk-forward ratings, out-of-sample MAE comparison, fitted market/model blend weight.More seasons of play-by-play to increase test power (currently 2024-2026).Backfill play-by-play from 1999 and refit; watch the blend weight.Promoting own lines above market-blind display.Answered for 2026 sample - see Lines page
Q14Which weather and venue effects are actually in the data?
Wind and rest change totals; missing weather silently biases totals.		Roof, surface, temperature and wind fields for completed games.Forecasts for upcoming games (fields are empty pre-game).Attach a forecast API at T-24h; keep the observed value for backtests.Totals model refinement in outdoor late-season games.Open
Q15Are travel and time-zone effects measurable?
A commonly claimed edge that needs geography, which the feed lacks.		Home/away and rest days only.Team base coordinates, kickoff local time, travel distance.Add a static stadium/timezone table; compute eastward travel and body-clock kickoff.Travel-based strategies.Open
Q16What is the correct benchmark for a 'top performing' strategy?
Ranking by raw ROI rewards variance and small samples.		Lower 95% ROI bound, BH q-value, season stability, drawdown, correlation.User agreement on weights between return and risk.Adopt the published scoring formula, or change the weights explicitly and re-rank.Reward allocation.Proposed - needs sign-off
Q17How often must data refresh, and who checks it?
Stale injury or line data silently degrades every decision.		Scheduled GitHub Actions refresh with pinned files and sha256 evidence.Alerting when a source changes schema or goes stale.Add a freshness check that fails the build and opens an issue when a feed is older than 24h.Trusting the dashboard in-week.Proposed
Q18Which bet types should the leaderboard score?
Singles, parlays and prop combos have different variance and different data needs.		Singles scored with gates; parlay payout maths and same-game correlation.Prop and parlay prices.Score singles only until a priced feed exists; publish parlay maths as education.Parlay/prop leaderboards.Proposed
Q19What happens when the model and the market disagree by a lot?
Large disagreement is usually model error, not a gift.		Distribution of model-minus-market edges and their realised ATS results.Enough large-edge samples to separate error from opportunity.Track edge buckets; require the bucket to be positive out of sample before betting it.Any 'actionable' label on the Lines page.Open - currently no bucket passes
Q20Who owns a suggestion once it is approved, and how is it reverted?
Suggestions without an owner and a rollback plan become permanent experiments.		Decision records with criteria, status and rollback notes.Named owners and a review cadence.Assign an owner per accepted suggestion; review at each data refresh.Nothing - process gap.Proposed
