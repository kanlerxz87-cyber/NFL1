Combos and correlation

A QB passing-yards leg and his WR receiving-yards leg move together; multiplying their probabilities assumes they do not. The Strategy page publishes measured correlation for game-level legs; player-level leg correlation needs game-level receiving data per target, which is not yet wired in. Until then, prop combos are published as model output only, never as a priced bet.
