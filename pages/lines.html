My Lines

Opponent-adjusted EPA ratings converted into a spread, a total and a win probability — then scored against the closing line.

Data built 2026-11-16T18:05:02+00:00 · every value links to its source

Method, in one paragraph

Two-way ridge fixed effect on non-garbage-time plays: EPA ~ offense team + defense team + home. Prior season weighted 0.4.

Ratings for week W use only weeks < W of the current season plus the previous season at 40% weight, so the backtest has no look-ahead. Margin model: a = 1.62, b = 27.93, residual SD = 13.38. Total model: intercept 45.48, offence coefficient 18.66, defence coefficient -5.63, residual SD 12.92.

Line sign convention (measured, not assumed)

Across 5604 games the correlation between the mirrored closing line and the home team's actual margin is 0.440. Positive correlation proves the mirrored closing line is expressed as points the HOME team is expected to win by (positive = home favourite).

This is why the first build produced an inverted comparison: the convention was assumed wrongly and then proved from the data. It is published here so a reviewer can challenge it. schedules release

Does the model add information beyond the closing line?

Regressing the actual margin on both the closing line and the model line (fit 2025, tested 2026) gives the market a weight of 1.336 and the model -0.328. Test MAE: hybrid 9.63 vs market-only 9.58 over 149 games.

A model weight at or below zero means the model's disagreement with the market was wrong on average in the test window; a positive weight with a lower test MAE means genuine incremental information. Weight on the model near zero means the EPA signal is already in the price; a positive weight with lower MAE is genuine incremental information. closing-line source
