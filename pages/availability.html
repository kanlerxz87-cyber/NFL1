Scope limit What this page can and cannot tell you

Can: name every player the official Week 10 report marks Out or Questionable, count them by unit for each team, and confirm which teams have a clean report.

Cannot: tell you whether an absent player was the starter, the 3rd-string backup, or whether his replacement is a downgrade. Official depth charts are not published — the depth-chart URL returns a 404 (checked 2026-11-12) — so the "starters vs backups" column is deliberately blank. Filling it would mean inventing the order.

Only rows where the official report states a game status are counted. Players listed with a practice status but no game status are NOT counted as out or available, because the source does not assert either.
