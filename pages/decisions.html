P1 · BlockerObtain a lawful, timestamped closing-line source before building anything else.Every decision the project cares about — edge, ROI, ATS accuracy, parlay value, leaderboard scoring — requires the price that was actually available. P1 · BlockerFix the measurement target: decide whether the leaderboard scores straight-up winners or spread performance.They are different games. This dataset shows a ~60% straight-up hit rate that is statistically indistinguishable from luck, while spread performance cannot be computed at all yet. Scoring the wrong metric rewards the wrong strategies.None in money; requires a written pre-registration so the metric cannot be changed after results are known.A signed-off metric definition, the minimum sample, and the promotion threshold, all fixed before the next season's data arrives.
P2 · HighAutomate the weekly capture so the sample grows without hand entry, and re-run verification on every update.The verdict on every strategy is currently limited by sample size (136 games). Automation converts each week into fresh out-of-sample evidence instead of one static snapshot.Engineering time; needs a permitted data channel because the current sandbox cannot reach the data host directly.A scheduled job that fetches, stores with timestamp and URL, re-runs the gate, and fails loudly if reconciliation breaks.
P2 · HighBuild the backup/attrition layer on participation snapshots, not depth charts.Official NFL depth charts are not published, so '2nd string' cannot be sourced. Who actually played — snapped counts and participation — is the defensible proxy for a starter being replaced.Requires a row-level participation feed; no official free source was found in this environment.For each injured starter, a dated record of the replacement and that player's snap share, linked to a source.
P3 · MediumPre-register a small number of hypotheses instead of testing many strategies.Four strategies were tested here and the best result stopped being significant after correction. Testing fewer, pre-declared ideas is the cheapest way to make a real edge detectable.Slower iteration; fewer headline numbers to show.A written hypothesis list with direction and threshold fixed in advance, and results reported for all of them, including failures.

Running paper-only first tests both the strategies and the scoring rules at zero financial risk.Prize funding, eligibility and jurisdiction review, audit and anti-fraud controls, tax treatment.A full paper season, locked predictions, independent audit, published rules, and a legal opinion before any money moves.
