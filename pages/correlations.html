Pearson r with a Fisher-z 95% confidence interval and a two-sided t test. A relationship is only published when at least 25 paired observations exist; otherwise it is reported as blank.

The three that decide everything

  • C1 = 0.4312. The closing spread already explains the result this well. That is the bar any own line has to beat, and it is why an unvalidated model line is not a signal.
  • C3 = 0.0190 (p = 0.113). Our disagreement with the closing line is not significantly correlated with the part of the result the market got wrong. This is the single most important number on the site: it is the reason no strategy is promoted.
  • C9 = -0.0923. Wind is the only environmental variable that moves the total at all, and it moves it down. Temperature does not (C8 = 0.0092, p = 0.517).

Sample

  • 7104 games, seasons 1999 to 2026.
  • 6976 carry both a closing spread and a closing total, so every figure above uses paired observations only.
  • Relationships with fewer than 25 paired observations are published as blank, never as a number.
