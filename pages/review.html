What a reviewer should decide first

  1. Buy a priced feed or stop claiming edge. Every strategy verdict depends on it.
  2. Accept the hybrid line as the default. It is the only line with measured out-of-sample support.
  3. Keep rewards on paper points. Legal review is outstanding.
  4. Ingest inactives and participation. The injury requirement cannot be finished without it.
