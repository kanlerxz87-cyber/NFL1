RESEARCH DESIGN · NO PERFORMANCE CLAIMS

Strategy lab

Strategy concepts are hypotheses, not validated edges. No success rate, correlation, expected value, or leaderboard ranking can be reported until a sourced dataset, market prices, and locked out-of-sample predictions exist.

Mandatory validation gates

1 · Provenance & preregistration

Define target, eligible markets, cutoff time, features, model, benchmark, exclusions, and primary metric before observing test outcomes. Archive immutable data snapshots and code versions.

2 · Leakage-safe time split

Train on earlier games, tune only on a later validation period, then evaluate once on untouched future seasons/weeks. Group all markets from a game together. Fit preprocessing only on training data.

3 · Probability quality & economics

Report log loss and Brier score versus a declared baseline; calibration plots; ROI/net units after actual quoted price and settlement; closing-line comparison only with defensible timestamps. Show uncertainty intervals.

4 · Robustness & multiplicity

Account for all tried strategies/features, repeated testing, season/regime variation, market dependence, and selection effects. Use bootstrap or other game-clustered uncertainty; disclose effective sample size and null comparisons.

5 · Paper stage & risk controls

Freeze model and stake rule, paper-track prospective picks with timestamps, record missed/void bets, cap exposure, track drawdown and calibration drift. Promotion thresholds must be set in advance.

6 · Leaderboard eligibility

Rank only after minimum sample and prospective validation criteria are specified; publish strategy version, bets, price timestamps, settled P&L, ROI, uncertainty, max drawdown, and audit trail. No real-money reward pool until legal, tax, eligibility, funding, and fair-play review.

Correlation and parlay treatment

Do not assume independence. Measure pairwise outcome dependence on aligned, out-of-sample game records; publish the metric (e.g. Pearson for continuous outcomes, phi for binary, or rank correlation), sample size, interval, conditioning set, and missing-data rule. Correlation is not causal and can change by matchup, role, and game state.

For parlays, model joint probability directly or estimate dependence explicitly; compare joint probability with offered payout and leg-by-leg baselines. A pairwise matrix alone does not determine higher-order joint probability. Same-game player props require explicit shared game-script effects. No correlation values are available in this repository: blank until calculated from validated records.

Official data entry points: NFL Stats, NFL Injury Reports, and NFL Football Operations. Sources do not establish any strategy as profitable.
