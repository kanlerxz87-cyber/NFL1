Null: the observed prices are fair, so a 1-unit bet at decimal d has mean profit 0 and variance d-1. The standard deviation of total profit is then sqrt(sum(d_i - 1)) over the bets actually placed. No simulation and no assumption beyond independence of bets. The last column is the number of bets the strategy would need before an edge of the observed size could be detected 80% of the time at the 5% level. Where that number is larger than any realistic season, the honest answer is that the sample cannot settle the question.

The eight gates

GateRequirementWhy it exists
G1walk-forward / pre-game information onlyEvery input to the bet decision is known before kick-off and, for the model rules, comes from a fit that used only earlier games.
G2observed prices on every scored betEvery scored bet carries a price taken from the source file. No -110 assumption is used.
G3at least 200 scored betsBelow this, a run of luck is indistinguishable from skill at any plausible effect size.
G4hit rate beats the break-even implied by the actual prices takenBreak-even is computed from the real price on each bet, not from 0.5.
G5survives multiple testing (BH-adjusted p < 0.05, 12 rules tested)Testing many rules and reporting only the best one manufactures winners; the Benjamini-Hochberg step charges for that.
G6positive in a untouched holdout (2024 + 2025)The holdout seasons were never used to tune the rule thresholds.
G7positive in at least 60% of individual seasonsAn edge that exists in only one season is a season, not a strategy.
G895% bootstrap confidence interval on ROI excludes zeroTen thousand resamples of the actual bet log; the interval must sit above zero.

8 gates, fixed before any strategy was evaluated. A strategy must clear all 8 to be called proven; the leaderboard publishes how many each one cleared.

What "removing luck" actually means here

  • A fair-price null, not a simulation. If the observed prices are fair, a 1-unit bet at decimal odds d has mean profit 0 and variance d-1. The 95% band on total profit is therefore ±1.96·sqrt(Σ(d-1)) over the bets actually placed.
  • Multiple testing. 12 rules were tested; every p-value is Benjamini-Hochberg corrected, so the best of a dozen rules cannot buy significance.
  • Holdout. 2024 and 2025 were never used to tune thresholds and are scored separately.
  • Power, not just significance. Where the bets needed for 80% power exceeds any realistic season, the honest answer is that this sample cannot settle the question.

What the sample cannot settle

  • 14440 bets scored across 4751 games.
  • 1902 games in the extract carry no published price. They are used for training and are never scored, because scoring them would require inventing a price.
  • Every scored bet carries an observed price (True). No vig assumption is applied.
