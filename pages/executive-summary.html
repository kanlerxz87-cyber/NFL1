Injury Tracking Focus

The system must track injuries and know deficiencies in defenses and offenses, including when 2nd/3rd/4th-string backups replace starters. Official injury reports are published weekly on nfl.com. Week 10 capture covers every row the official report marks with a game status — 6 players (5 Out, 1 Questionable) across New England, Washington and the Giants — plus 4 teams explicitly reporting no injuries. Clubs with no captured report are omitted, not shown healthy. See Injuries page.

Notable Week 10 injuries captured: Lucas Patrick (Commanders, C, Concussion, Out), Colson Yankoff (Commanders, TE, Knee, Out), Malik Harrison (Giants, LB, Groin, Questionable).
