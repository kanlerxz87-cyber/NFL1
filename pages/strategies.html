How a strategy earns a place on the board

Each rule is scored on every graded bet, then forced through eight gates. Status is PASS only when all eight hold, WATCH at five or more, FAIL below that.

GateRule
G1Every input file pinned by URL and sha256.
G2Rule fixed before evaluation; no threshold search.
G3At least 100 graded bets.
G4Positive ROI across the two most recent seasons.
G5Benjamini-Hochberg q < 0.10 against break-even win rate.
G6At least 60% of tested seasons positive.
G7Max drawdown no worse than -25 units.
G8|correlation| with any other strategy below 0.7.

Parlays and correlation

Across 5592 games, the correlation between the home team's margin and the game total is 0.017, and the same-game link between "home covers" and "over hits" is 0.009 (phi coefficient).

Same-game legs are not independent. Use the phi value to adjust a 2-leg same-game parlay: P = p1p2 + rho*sqrt(p1(1-p1)p2(1-p2)). With near-zero phi, a same-game spread/total parlay is close to independent and the naive break-even is roughly right; with a large |phi| the naive number either flatters or punishes the bet. source

Rewards: points, not prizes

Points are awarded for statistically defensible performance, not raw ROI: 100 x max(0, lower 95% ROI bound) + 20 x seasons positive + 10 x gates passed. Strategies failing G3 (n<100) score zero. No cash, no prizes: this is a paper competition. Legal review is required before any real reward.

Statuses: {'PASS': 'all gates passed', 'WATCH': '5-7 gates passed', 'FAIL': 'fewer than 5 gates passed'}. Legal review is required before any reward with monetary value; see Flagged for Review.
