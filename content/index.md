## What this is

A verified-data-first research system for the NFL. It collects official league data, checks
it line by line, computes only what the data supports, and refuses to publish anything it
cannot source. It is built to originate betting lines and to rank candidate strategies on a
leaderboard - and today it correctly reports that it cannot yet do either, because the two
datasets an edge is measured against are not obtainable from this environment.

Nothing on this site is a betting recommendation, and no value has been estimated to fill a gap.
