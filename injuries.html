Cross-check with depth charts

The Depth Charts page transcribes the 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th string for offense and defense from official club sites. Combining those columns with the official injury designations and the official inactives list is the intended way to detect a 2nd/3rd/4th-string replacement. Because two clubs label their charts "Unofficial" and the league does not publish depth rank, no deficiency value is computed yet.
