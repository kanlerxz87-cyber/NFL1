How this answers the requirement

The brief asks the system to know deficiencies in defenses when 2nd, 3rd or 4th-string backups replace starters, and the same for offenses. The official club charts above are the only reviewed official source that publishes string order; the official inactives list and injury report establish who is unavailable. Joining these three official surfaces yields the replacement depth rank. The resulting deficiency score is deliberately blank because no official source publishes an effect size, and the brief forbids inferring one.
