Overview

A working dashboard for originating NFL lines, punishing luck, and choosing what to change next — with a review-and-decide surface so no change is implemented before someone has looked at the options.

Built 2026-11-13 12:45 UTC · data captured 2026-11-13T12:05Z–12:30Z · season 2026, week 10 complete · every table row links to its source

Read this first. The laboratory's methods hold up under independent recomputation, and today's scheduled refresh closed a standings drift that this session found on arrival: the site now matches the official page on all 32 clubs and 137 games. What remains is not a data-entry problem. The injury table's fallback to week-6 statuses is mislabelled, two leaderboards disagree, the priced board's sample cannot be reconciled with the file it was computed from, and 90% of the depth-chart evidence collected is unpublished. None of the twelve strategy rules passes its own gates.

What we know

  • The official 2026 standings reconcile arithmetically and now match the site: 32 clubs, 137 games, wins equal losses, ties in pairs, points for equal points against, and 0 clubs drifting from today's official page after the 12:51Z refresh.
  • The committed 296-row Week 10 injury scrape matches today's official nfl.com report club by club, with zero row-count differences on every club that could be read to its last row.
  • nfl.com publishes no depth chart: the club URL returns a soft 404. The 32 club sites publish their own charts and several label them 'Unofficial'.
  • The priced strategy board reproduces exactly from committed data. S07 (fade the road favourite of 7+) is 401 bets, 55.58% hit rate, +24.318 units, 6.064% ROI - identical to the published row.
  • No rule passes all eight validation gates on either board. The best priced rule has p=0.198 before multiple-testing correction.

What we do not know

  • Whether the nflverse closing lines are a consensus or a single book - nfl.com publishes no odds, so there is nothing official to check them against (Q-18).
  • The exact filter behind the published correlation sample: r reproduces, n does not (Q-16, F-109).
  • Which players actually dressed: no inactive list is captured for any week (Q-09).
  • When each club last updated its depth chart; only 3 of 31 pages carry a date (Q-08).
  • Whether HOU has a depth chart at all (Q-07).
