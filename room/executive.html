What we know

  • The official 2026 standings reconcile arithmetically: 32 clubs, 137 games, wins equal losses, ties in pairs, points for equal points against. Captured today and stored with its URL.
  • The committed 296-row Week 10 injury scrape matches today's official nfl.com report club by club, with zero row-count differences on every club that could be read to its last row.
  • nfl.com publishes no depth chart: the club URL returns a soft 404. The 32 club sites publish their own charts and several label them 'Unofficial'.
  • The priced strategy board reproduces exactly from committed data. S07 (fade the road favourite of 7+) is 401 bets, 55.58% hit rate, +24.318 units, 6.064% ROI - identical to the published row.
  • No rule passes all eight validation gates on either board. The best priced rule has p=0.198 before multiple-testing correction.
  • The own-line model does not beat the market: 10.40 vs 9.72 points of mean absolute spread error in the 2025 validation season, with an interval that excludes zero.
  • Of 66 scored parlay pairs, exactly one survives multiple-testing correction, and the published reading correctly attributes it to the parlay pricing rule rather than skill.
  • No player-prop price exists in any source, so no prop bet can be scored and no prop ROI is published.
  • The leaderboard's ordering matches its caption: the table is sorted by the lower 95% ROI bound, as the page says. An earlier draft of this review called it a defect; the claim was withdrawn and is recorded as F-116.

What we do not know

  • Whether the nflverse closing lines are a consensus or a single book - nfl.com publishes no odds, so there is nothing official to check them against (Q-18).
  • The exact filter behind the published correlation sample: r reproduces, n does not (Q-16, F-109).
  • Which players actually dressed: no inactive list is captured for any week (Q-09).
  • When each club last updated its depth chart; only 3 of 31 pages carry a date (Q-08).
  • Whether HOU has a depth chart at all (Q-07).
  • What a prop price would be, and whether any licensed feed is affordable (Q-11).
  • What the competition rewards; no official source publishes a reward value (Q-13).
  • Whether using any of this to bet is legal where the reader lives (Q-20).
  • How the season ends - every forward-looking number on the site is a modelled distribution, not a forecast with a track record.

What is wrong with the current site

  • Standings drift: the site shows NYG 5-2-1 and WAS 2-5-1; the official page shows 5-3-1 and 3-5-1 after Thursday's 24-21 result (F-101, material).
  • Two leaderboards: one assumes -110 on every bet, the other uses observed prices, and they rank different rules under the same names (F-102, material).
  • The depth-chart page shows 3 of the 31 captured clubs (F-104, material).
  • The priced board's totals cannot be reconciled with the committed extract: 6,653 candidate games published, 5,471 in the file (F-114, minor).
  • Missing prices are concentrated in 2006, 2008 and the unfinished 2026 season, so the exclusion is a selection effect (F-115, minor).
  • The market column cites an official page that publishes no odds (F-106, minor).
  • Blank injury designations read as healthy (F-105, minor).
  • Two generators write index.html and the pull-request check audits the one that is not served (F-107, minor).
  • Several site generations publish overlapping numbers (F-103, minor).
