Review and choose a path

Decide

Every suggestion, restated as a decision with options. Nothing here has been implemented: the page exists so a human can pick a path before any change is made.

Built 2026-11-13 12:50 UTC · data captured 2026-11-13T12:05Z–12:30Z · season 2026, week 10 complete · every table row links to its source

  1. Read the question and the options. Each option states what it buys, what it costs and what it risks.
  2. Open the evidence on the option you prefer. Every link goes to an official page or to the committed data the claim is computed from.
  3. Choose a path and record it. The gate under the card is the acceptance test the change has to pass before it ships.
  4. Only then implement. Nothing on this page has been applied to the site.

12 decisions are ready for a choice and 2 are blocked until a named piece of information exists. Findings and unverified values never block a decision; they travel with it to the Flagged for review page.

D-02 Retire the -110 price assumption before any strategy is presented as a candidateStrategy leaderboard · 1 renderer + 1 data file; no analysis rewrite ready to decide

Two leaderboards are live: one assumes -110 on every bet, the other uses observed prices. Which one should be the only one on the site?

AObserved-price board onlyrecommended
Buys
Every scored bet carries the price the source recorded; the ROI is achievable by definition at that price.
Costs
Parts of the file carry no price, so 1,902 of 6,653 priced-or-unpriced games are excluded and must be published as excluded.
Risk
Smaller samples per rule; several rules fall below 200 bets and therefore cannot pass any gate.
Evidence
  • Observed-price board 14,440 scored bets, 12 rules
  • Price rule (G2) 'No -110 assumption is used'
B-110 board only
Buys
A much longer window (2005-2026) and bigger samples.
Costs
Every ROI on the page is an assumption, not an observed return; a real book prices most of these at worse than -110.
Risk
Overstates returns by construction; the assumption is documented but the headline number is not achievable.
Evidence
  • -110 board window.price_assumption = '-110 on every bet'
CKeep both, labelled and separated
Buys
Continuity with earlier pages; both methodologies stay visible.
Costs
Two rows can carry the same rule name and different ROI, which is the current state.
Risk
Reader confusion; this is exactly what produced the conflicting numbers today.
Evidence
  • Homepage leaderboard S11 'Under in divisional games with total 47+' 4.2% (-110 board)
  • Research board the closest rule is S09 'Under when the total is 40 or lower' at -7.9%
Recommendation A — A leaderboard competition is only meaningful if the number it ranks is a return someone could have earned. The -110 board can stay as a labelled sensitivity table, never as the headline.
Pre-implementation gateGate: no strategy may appear in the headline table without a non-null average decimal price and a count of excluded bets on the same row.
