Correlations

Coefficients computed only from official numbers, each with its sample size and interval, and blanked below the publication floor.

A correlation is published here only when both of its inputs are official numbers, and never without its sample size and a 95% interval. The first table uses one source for both columns: the league standings page. The second uses the mirrored results feed and is printed only above a 25-observation floor; below the floor the coefficient is left blank rather than shown with a warning nobody reads.

What a coefficient here does not mean

It is not a prediction

That wins correlate with points differential is arithmetic, not a forecast. It says the standings table is internally coherent.

It is not a strategy

No correlation on this page was tested on a holdout, so none of them is evidence that a rule makes money.

It is not causal

A high r between two league-published totals says the two move together. It says nothing about which one moves the other.

It is checkable

Every number was computed in the build step from the stored official rows; the verification gate recomputes the interval and fails if r falls outside it.
