Decision

The single highest-value next step is not a model. It is a result store. Until a finished game has a dated, sourced final, every strategy on this site is a claim about the past that nobody can score. The second is the price policy, because a residual against an unsourced closing line describes the market without measuring anything. Both are small pieces of work with outsized consequences, and both are recorded as decisions on the Prices and Results pages.
