Market Book

Our fair line, the market line, and the residual between them, as separate columns for every Week-11 fixture.

Origination order is fixed: the side is priced first, then the total, then the moneyline. Each market gets three columns — our fair number, the market number, and the residual between them — so a reader never has to subtract two figures to find the claim. Positive residual = our number is higher than the market's on the home team. The spread and total columns are signed from the home team's point of view throughout, and the residual is computed from the printed numbers, not re-typed.
