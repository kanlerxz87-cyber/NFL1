Suggestions

Ten options, ranked by how much they unblock, each with its evidence and its cost.

Suggestions are options for a reviewer, not changes. Nothing on this page has been implemented; each one names the evidence it rests on and what it would cost. The top four are ordered by how much of the rest of the work they unblock, not by how interesting they are.

How to choose one

Every suggestion above is an option, not a change. Nothing on this site was built from a suggestion without a pre-registration, and no suggestion is implemented by the page that proposes it. To pick one, read the decision row it maps to on Readiness, then the question it depends on. The four questions that gate the most work are Q01 (results), Q02 (prices), Q03 (rule identity) and Q04 (point-in-time availability).

Each suggestion maps to a row on Readiness and to a question on Questions. A reviewer who wants the largest single gain should start with SG01; a reviewer who wants the site to stop overstating itself should start with SG02.
