Strategies

Every rule on both boards: the signal, the price it had to beat, the money it made, and the identity problem between the two files.

Three questions are asked about every rule, and they have different answers. Is there a signal? compares the win rate with a coin flip. Does it survive the price? compares the same win rate with the break-even rate the price implies. Did it make money? is the board's own ROI. A rule can pass the first and fail the second, which is exactly why a staking number on its own is not evidence of skill.

Luck, stated as arithmetic

The repository's own null model: at fair prices a one-unit bet at decimal odds d has zero mean profit and variance d − 1, so the total profit of a run of bets has a standard deviation of √Σ(di − 1). A profit inside that band is what luck alone produces. The power calculation is the same idea from the other end: 4,904 graded bets would be needed to detect a 52% win rate against a 50% break-even at 80% power, which is about 377 weeks at 13 games a week.
