What this page refuses to do

Fill a blank

If no source read this session confirms a value, the cell says Unverified. There is no interpolation and no "likely".

Infer a starter

A club chart nominates a backup. That is not a start. The replacement column stays blank until a game book says otherwise.

Call a consensus close a price

The mirrored feed is a consensus number. It is labelled that way in every column and every note.

Quote a correlation without n

Every coefficient is printed with its sample size and interval, and is blanked below the 25-observation floor.
