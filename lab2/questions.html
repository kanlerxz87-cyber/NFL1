Questions that cannot be answered at all today

Who actually started?

No game book is stored, and no source read this session names a starter. Every table on the site that could name a replacement leaves the cell blank for this reason.

What did the market open at?

No opening number exists in any file the repository holds. A model of price movement cannot be tested without one.

Did last week's forecast win?

No Week-11 game has been played, and the Week-10 results were missing from the feed that would score it. Q01 is the blocking question.

Is any rule actually better than chance?

Not at the multiplicity-corrected level on the sample that exists: 0 of 24 rules have q < 0.05, and a 52% edge would need about seven seasons of games to prove.
