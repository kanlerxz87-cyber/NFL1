Purpose

This repository exists to collect, organise and analyse NFL information well enough to originate its own betting lines, and to do so under an audit trail where every published number can be traced to an official source or is visibly blank.

This page is the short version. Everything on it is reproduced with full working, source links and verification status on the linked pages.

What was in the repository before this session

One commit and one file: README.md, containing the six characters # NFL1. There was no code, no data, no site and no task board. Everything described below was built in this session, so the "repo review" task had no pre-existing content to verify - that is itself recorded as a finding rather than quietly assumed away.

Real analysis produced from verified data

These are computations, not estimates, and each is reproducible from the cited source.

  • Fitted Pythagorean exponent: 3.5073 (95% bootstrap CI 2.7498-4.3113, n=32,

R²=0.7715, calibration slope 0.9991). Fitted by least squares on the 32 official rows, not borrowed from any external study. The interval is wide and the exponent is high because it is fitted on roughly 8.5 games per team rather than a full season - stated as a limitation, not hidden.

  • League home win rate: 49.63% (Wilson 95% CI 41.36%-57.93%, n=136 home games). Home

teams are not winning more than half their games in 2026 so far. The interval includes 50%, so this does not yet establish that home advantage has disappeared - it establishes that the usual ~2.5-point home-field assumption cannot be asserted from this data.

  • Luck table: the gap between each team's Pythagorean expectation and its actual

winning percentage, expressed in games. Tampa Bay is the most under-performing team relative to its scoring (+2.48 games of bad luck); Denver (-2.01) and Cleveland (-2.00) are the most over-performing.

  • Correlation screen: 38 hypothesis tests across 21 signals and 2 targets, with

Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery-rate control. At p<0.05 alone, 1.9 false positives would be expected purely by chance. Nine tests survive FDR control at α=0.10 - and all nine are definitional, relating win percentage to other expressions of the same game outcomes. Zero non-mechanical findings survive multiple-testing control. That is the honest headline, and it is the single most important number on this site.

What the injury data can and cannot support

The Week 10 report is real and complete, but it was retrieved on the Thursday of game week, and at that point it is barely populated:

  • Injury designation coverage: 2.36% - only 6 of 254 entries name a body part.
  • Game-status coverage: 2.36% - only 6 entries carry Out/Doubtful/Questionable, all of

them for the two games with the earliest reporting deadlines.

  • Practice participation is fully populated: 93 DNP, 89 LP, 72 FP.

Consequence for operations: practice status is usable early in the week, but designations are not. Any availability model must re-fetch after the Friday 4:00pm ET deadline, and the system is built to timestamp each retrieval so a strategy can never be evaluated against information that was not yet public.

Separately, and importantly: NFL.com does not publish a public depth chart. The league's sanctioned weekly depth-chart feed is Sportradar's NFL Official API, which requires an API key. String level - whether a 2nd, 3rd or 4th string backup is replacing a starter - is therefore reported as UNAVAILABLE and never guessed. The specific starter changes that are officially confirmed are captured from NFL.com news instead.

Line origination status

The origination board for all 14 Week 10 games is published on the Week 10 Board page. It shows what can honestly be computed and what cannot.

Published (DERIVED - arithmetic on verified cells): rating gap between the two clubs, Pythagorean expectation for each club, and a naive total equal to the sum of the two clubs' verified points-per-game.

Left blank on purpose: point spread, betting total, win probability, market line and every measure of edge. Two inputs are missing and neither can be invented:

  1. Home-field advantage expressed in points. Needs game-level final scores with

home/away designation across a season.

  1. Closing market lines. No licensed odds or market-data provider is reachable or

credentialed from this environment.

The engine is written to refuse: when a required input is None the dependent output is None plus a named blocking input, rather than a plausible-looking number. No line on this site is a betting recommendation.

Strategy programme status

Thirteen strategies are catalogued: four singles, two totals, three parlays, two player props and two prop combos. Each states its hypothesis, its required inputs, its skill basis and how luck is to be removed from its evaluation.

Every one of the thirteen currently returns NOT_STAKEABLE. The leaderboard shows 0 scored, 13 withheld. Scores are withheld rather than zeroed, so "not yet measured" can never be confused with "measured and worthless".

Nine validation gates stand between a strategy and a stake. They are implemented and unit-tested: the harness demonstrably approves a well-formed strategy and rejects one with lookahead, one whose edge is within noise, and one whose profit comes from a single fold. Today they all block, because there are no settled wagers, no odds history and no timestamped feature history.

The three things that unblock everything

  1. A closing-odds feed - at least three full seasons of closing spreads, totals and

moneylines, plus opening lines so closing-line value can be measured. Without this there is no ROI, no edge, no CLV and no leaderboard.

  1. Official depth charts - Sportradar's NFL Official API, or an equivalent sanctioned

feed. Without this, string level cannot be attached to any absence.

  1. Game-level results and play-by-play - to fit home-field advantage in points, to run

walk-forward backtests, and to build EPA-based ratings that are far stronger than points-per-game.

Each is specified with the exact source that would resolve it on the Open Questions page, and each has a corresponding item on the Suggestions Board.

Bottom line

The system, the verification discipline and the site are complete and working, and the current-season data is genuinely verified against official sources. What does not exist yet is any evidence that a betting edge exists, because the two datasets an edge is measured against - closing odds and depth charts - are not obtainable from this environment. Nothing on this site should be staked on, and no number has been invented to make it look otherwise.
