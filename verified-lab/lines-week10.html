Why the spread column is empty

Requires game-level final scores with home/away designation for a full season or more. NFL.com publishes these only on per-week score pages, which were not obtainable within this session's budget; the NFL's bulk feed and the nflverse release assets are both unreachable from the build environment's network allowlist.

Resolving source: https://www.nfl.com/scores/2026/week-1 .. week-9 (official) or https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data release 'schedules' (games.csv)
