Attempted and not reachable

Recording what could not be reached matters as much as recording what was. The build environment allows outbound connections only to github.com, api.github.com, codeload.github.com, registry.npmjs.org, pypi.org and files.pythonhosted.org. Data collection was therefore carried out through the agent's page-retrieval tool against official sources, and bulk historical feeds were not obtainable.
