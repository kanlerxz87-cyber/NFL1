Why every score is blank. A leaderboard rank is computed from a t-statistic on no-vig return on investment. That requires settled wagers and a closing-odds feed, and neither exists. Blanking the score rather than zeroing it is deliberate: a zero would mean "measured and worthless", which is a different claim from "not yet measured", and confusing the two is how bad strategies get kept and good ones get thrown away.

Scoring formula

leaderboard_score = t_statistic(no_vig_roi) x stability_multiplier x capacity_factor
  t_statistic          = mean(no-vig ROI) / (sd(returns) / sqrt(n))
  stability_multiplier = share of leave-one-week-out and leave-one-team-out
                           folds that retain the sign of the edge, clipped to [0,1]
  capacity_factor      = min(1, average posted limit / model stake)
Gating: score is only published when gates G1-G7 PASS. A strategy that is
NOT_STAKEABLE or REJECTED shows a blank score, never a zero, so that 'not
measured' is never confused with 'measured and worthless'.
Ranking on a t-statistic rather than raw ROI is deliberate. Raw ROI ranks noise: a strategy returning 80% on eleven wagers outranks one returning 9% on nine hundred, which is exactly backwards. The t-statistic forces a strategy to be both good and proven, and sample size enters the score directly.
