How to read this page

A decision is only as good as the information behind it. This page lists every question that must be answered before a betting, modelling or product decision can reasonably be made, and separates for each one what is already known and verified, what is not known, and the concrete action that would discover it.

Nothing here is rhetorical. Each question names the decision it blocks. Where the answer is already in hand it is stated with its source. Where it is not, the cell is blank rather than filled with a plausible guess.

Priority key: P0 blocks the entire programme, P1 blocks a category of work, P2 improves quality but is not blocking.

Do not have: any statistically significant association between an availability signal and an outcome.
