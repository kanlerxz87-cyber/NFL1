What this session verified, line by line

  • Standings. All 32 clubs read from https://www.nfl.com/standings/league/2026/REG on 2026-11-16. The table is arithmetically self-consistent: wins equal losses at 148 each and points for equal points against at 6,880. The Monday-night fixture had not kicked off, so 149 of a possible 160 games are in it.
  • Reconciliation. Recomputing the table from every game result in the feed plus the 12 Week-10 finals transcribed from the official schedule page reproduces 32 of 32 clubs cell by cell — W, L, T, points for and points against.
  • Injury report. The whole official page re-read on 2026-11-16: 347 rows, 28 clubs, 14 fixtures, 0 rows only on one side and 0 field differences against the 2026-11-15 capture. The sha256 of this session's rows file equals the hash already recorded in data/capture/manifest.json, so the earlier capture is faithful and the page did not move in the interval.
  • Lions designations. Kerby Joseph, Cade Mays and D.J. Reed are published with a blank game status in one hand copy still inside the repository; the official page lists all three as Out. Re-confirmed this session.
  • The reserve list exists. The league publishes a dated reserve list, and no session had captured it. This session did: 25 players across 17 clubs for November 2026. 15 of the 25 do not appear in the Week 10 injury report at all, including Malik Willis — the exact example the brief uses. Every per-team injury count printed without it has been understating unavailability. See Injuries & depth.
  • Roster turnover has an official source. nfl.com/transactions publishes trades, signings, the reserve list, waivers, terminations and other movements by month. That is what a staleness rule for depth rows needs.
  • Week 11. The Week 11 injury report is not published. The reg11 URL serves the Week 10 page. There is no official forward-looking availability data today, so no Week-11 line may carry an injury feature.
