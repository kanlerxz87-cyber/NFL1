Suggestions & choose a path

Improvements to the site and to decision making. Each carries the paths a reviewer can pick from; nothing is built until a path is chosen here.

Review and choose a path — before anything is implemented

Nothing here is implemented on the strength of this page. Each suggestion carries two or three paths with their cost, risk and what they unlock. A reviewer records a choice in console/data/decisions.json; only then does the work start. Until a choice is recorded the status is 'awaiting review'.

0 of 20 suggestions have a recorded choice. The rest are awaiting review, and no work starts on them.

How to record a choice

Edit this file: set decisions[<suggestion id>] = {"chosen_path": "<the path label>", "chosen_by": "<name>", "chosen_on": "<YYYY-MM-DD>", "note": "<optional>"}. Then rebuild with console/build.py. The Suggestions page renders the choice and the build fails if a chosen path is not one of the published options.

The build fails if a recorded choice is not one of the published options, so a typo cannot silently become a decision.
