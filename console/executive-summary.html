Executive summary

What the evidence supports, what it does not, what is missing, and the choices only a human can make.

What holds up

  • The official data is reproducible. 32 of 32 clubs in the official standings table are reproduced cell by cell from game results — no unexplained residual anywhere in the table.
  • The injury capture is faithful. 347 rows re-read from https://www.nfl.com/injuries/league/2026/reg10 match the committed capture row for row and byte for byte.
  • The price policy is real. Every scored bet carries the price actually taken from the source file; no −110 assumption is used anywhere, and every downloaded asset is pinned by sha256.
  • The sample is large. 7,104 games and 6,976 with both lines, which is what makes the correlation and holdout work meaningful at all.
  • Luck is charged for. Ranking uses a benchmark-relative score with a bootstrap interval and a Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment across 12 tested rules, on an untouched 2024+2025 holdout.

What does not hold up

  • Nothing is promotable. 0 of 12 strategies clear every gate that can be machine-checked from the published fields (5 of 8 gates are checkable; the rest cannot be evaluated from what the analysis prints). Every number on the board is retrospective and no strategy has a prospective sample, so even a clean sweep would only make a rule a candidate for one.
  • The results feed is 12 games stale. It was taken before the Week 10 Sunday kickoffs. Any weekly number computed from it today describes last week.
  • Three of four leaderboard divisions are empty and should be: no combination bet may be ranked until a joint probability is validated on held-out data and the operator's void/push rules are stored.
  • The injury signal cannot be a change yet. Three dated captures exist, all taken on a Saturday, Sunday and Monday. There is no within-week series to difference.
  • Depth is unresolved. 149 slots list more than one rank-1 player, two clubs fail to parse and one publishes its chart as an image. The deficiency matrix is blocked until one route is verified for all 32 clubs.

What is missing

  • Opening prices. The store is closing-only, so the move is invisible.
  • Venues, from the official game centre. Nothing in the repository holds one.
  • Reserve lists. "Not listed" still cannot be distinguished from "on injured reserve".
  • Game Books, inactive lists and snap counts for the current week, so a backup starting for a starter can be suspected but not confirmed.
  • Any measured meaning for "Questionable". The official text publishes no percentage and none has been measured.

Where the numbers come from

Official pages read on 2026-11-16: standings, schedule and results, Week 10 injury report. Historical results, prices, injuries and player weeks: nflverse (CC-BY-4.0), pinned by sha256 in data/s9cb/provenance.json. The league publishes no official odds; closing lines in the feed are consensus numbers and are labelled as such.

Every row on this site links to the official page it was read from. A value no official page publishes is printed as a labelled blank, never inferred. Built by console/build.py; checked by console/verify.py. Research tool, not betting advice.
