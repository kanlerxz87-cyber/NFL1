This sub-site publishes the venue evidence for the NFL1 project: stadium transcription for Weeks 10 and 11, roof/surface registers read from official NFL Game Books, an official NOAA/NWS hourly forecast overlay around each Week-11 kickoff, and the dated 2026 schedule announcement shown beside the live schedule.

Executive summary

  • 14 Week-10 and 13 Week-11 venues transcribed from official nfl.com game-centre pages, each row linked to its page: Week 10 · Week 11.
  • 12 of 13 Week-11 venues have roof and surface transcribed verbatim from official NFL Game Books; 1 is blank and flagged (Estadio Banorte — no completed Game Book exists yet).
  • 11 of 13 Week-11 venues have official NWS hourly wind/temperature/precipitation values for the kickoff window; 2 flagged (outside NWS coverage; kickoff beyond the NWS hourly horizon at read time).
  • Every published value links the official source it was read from. Unconfirmable values stay blank and are labelled UNVERIFIED/FLAGGED — see Flagged for review.

Method

  1. Venues: printed Location and Stadium fields from the official NFL game-centre pages (read_at per row in the data files).
  2. Roof/surface/weather: page 1 of the official Game Book (PDF) of each club's most recent completed home game at that venue; conditions copied verbatim.
  3. NWS overlay: stadium coordinates resolved via OpenStreetMap Nominatim (query input only), then NOAA api.weather.gov /points and forecast/hourly — all published weather values are NWS values with the gridpoint URL on each row.
  4. Schedule announcement: dated nfl.com article, quoted verbatim on schedule.html.
