How luck is removed →

Every rule is fixed before evaluation and must clear all eight gates. The closest is S07 (fade the road favourite of 7+): 6.06% ROI over 401 bets, 6 of 8 gates, and a 95% luck band of -37.2 to +37.2 units.

Our line adds something, but is worse

Our spread error is 10.70 points against the market's 10.24, yet the disagreement between them still carries information (weight 0.161, t = 3.35). Both facts are published; neither is enough to call a signal.
