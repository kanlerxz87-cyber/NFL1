What this project is

NFL1 collects, organises and analyses a full list of requirements for an NFL decision-intelligence system, then publishes every finding as a source-linked table. The site covers decision questions and missing information, improvement suggestions with a choose-a-path gate, own-line research and signal strategies with validation gates and correlation values, injury and backup-depth tracking, an executive summary, verification evidence and a clearly labelled Flagged for Review page.

The data snapshot is the 2026 NFL regular season, Week 10, retrieved on 2026-11-12 (UTC) from official league, club, government and platform sources. Every table row links to the official page the value came from. Where an official source publishes no value, the cell is left blank and the row is flagged. No value is inferred or filled in.
