## Verification result

`tools/verify_combined.py` compares all 32 official standings rows in
`data/standings.csv` and `data/verified/standings_2026.csv` against
`data/standings.json`, field by field (W, L, T, PCT, PF, PA, NET). It also recomputes
W/L/T, PF, PA and NET from the 136 official game results in
`data/verified/games_2026.csv`. Results are published on `integration.html`.

The project does **not** publish the ordinal `rank` column as an official statistic:
the NFL standings page has no rank column (flag F25). The two project snapshots use
different abbreviations for the Rams (`LA` and `LAR`), both preserved and flagged F29.
