What this board is for

Nothing on this page has been implemented. Each item is a proposal, written so a reviewer can read the reasoning, weigh the cost against the benefit, and choose a path before any code changes. The Decision column is deliberately blank and is the reviewer's to fill in.

To decide an item, record one of: Approve, Reject, Defer, or Needs more information (naming what). Approved items become tasks on COORDINATION.md with an owner and a file scope. Rejected items stay on the board with the reason recorded, so the same idea is not silently re-proposed later.

Effort is the reviewer's cost in focused working sessions: S = under half a day, M = one to three days, L = a week or more, XL = depends on an external contract or vendor. Impact is how much better a decision would be afterwards: 1 = marginal, 3 = material, 5 = unblocks work that is currently impossible.

A. Data acquisition - the work that unblocks everything else

S-01 · Connect a licensed closing-odds feed

Effort XL · Impact 5 · Priority P0 · Decision: ______ Nothing about betting can be measured without market prices. Three full seasons of closing spreads, totals and moneylines - plus opening lines, so closing-line value can be measured - is the minimum credible history. Closing-line value is the single most reliable early indicator of genuine skill, because it is measurable long before ROI accumulates enough sample to be statistically meaningful. The arithmetic to consume such a feed already exists and is unit-tested in src/nfl/odds.py; only the connection is missing.

S-02 · Obtain official depth-chart credentials

Effort L · Impact 5 · Priority P0 · Decision: ______ Sportradar's NFL Official API is the NFL's sanctioned weekly depth-chart feed. Without it, string level is unknown for every player in the dataset and the specific requirement - knowing when a 2nd, 3rd or 4th string backup replaces a starter - cannot be met by any honest means. Scraping 32 club websites is the fallback, but charts there are unversioned and inconsistently formatted, which makes them unsuitable for gate G3's no-lookahead requirement.

S-03 · Build a game-level results archive from official per-week score pages

Effort L · Impact 5 · Priority P0 · Decision: ______ Game-by-game final scores with home/away designation are needed to fit home-field advantage in points, to run walk-forward backtests, and to validate any rating model. The official per-week score pages are reachable but verbose, so this should be a scheduled retriever writing compact structured rows, not a one-off manual transcription.

S-04 · Snapshot the injury report on a schedule, every version, timestamped

Effort M · Impact 5 · Priority P0 · Decision: ______ The current snapshot proves the problem: retrieved on the Thursday of game week, only 2.36% of entries carried an injury designation and 2.36% carried a game status. Practice participation was fully populated. That means an early-week snapshot and a Friday-evening snapshot describe materially different worlds. Capturing Wednesday, Thursday, Friday post-deadline and Saturday versions produces the first real measurement of information arrival - which is itself a tradeable signal, and is a hard prerequisite for gate G3.

S-05 · Add a per-retrieval content hash and a change log

Effort S · Impact 3 · Priority P1 · Decision: ______ Every snapshot should record a hash of its raw content and a diff against the previous version. This makes it possible to prove later that a value was available at a given moment, which is the difference between a defensible backtest and an unfalsifiable one. It also catches silent source-format changes before they corrupt the data.

S-06 · Re-retrieve the three inconsistent standings cells from a second official view

Effort S · Impact 3 · Priority P1 · Decision: ______ The DIV records for Indianapolis and Philadelphia and the NON-CONF record for Washington each contradict their own adjacent percentage cell. In all three cases the printed cell is character-identical to a neighbouring cell, which points at a rendering duplication defect rather than a real record. Re-retrieving the parameterised division view and cross-checking against the conference view would establish whether the defect is in the source or in the retrieval. Until then the cells stay excluded and flagged, which is correct but leaves a known hole.

B. Modelling

S-07 · Replace points-per-game ratings with an EPA-based rating

Effort L · Impact 4 · Priority P1 · Decision: ______ Net points per game is the best rating obtainable from the standings alone, and it is a coarse instrument: it treats a 40-point blowout and a 3-point win as different amounts of the same thing, and it says nothing about whether a score came against a first-string defence in the first quarter or a prevent unit in the fourth. Expected points added per play is materially stronger and is the standard in serious NFL modelling. It requires play-by-play, which is currently unreachable from this environment.

S-08 · Fit a proper Elo or state-space rating instead of a season-to-date average

Effort M · Impact 4 · Priority P1 · Decision: ______ A season-to-date average weights Week 1 as heavily as Week 9. An Elo-style rating with a recency factor, or a Kalman filter over game margins, tracks a team's current strength far better and handles roster turnover mid-season. Needs S-03 first.

S-09 · Fit the availability weight tables instead of assuming them

Effort M · Impact 4 · Priority P1 · Decision: ______ The severity weights (Out 1.00, Doubtful 0.80, Questionable 0.50, DNP 0.45, LP 0.25, FP 0.05) and the position weights (QB 1.00 down to FB 0.10) are analyst priors, published in full on the Methodology page and labelled MODELED everywhere they appear. They should be fitted against observed line movement once S-01 and S-02 exist. Until then every number that depends on them is a ranking aid, not an estimate.

S-10 · Model totals from team-level scoring rates rather than summing points per game

Effort M · Impact 3 · Priority P2 · Decision: ______ The published naive total is the arithmetic sum of two clubs' verified points-per-game. It is honest and it is reproducible, but it ignores pace, opponent strength, venue and weather, and it double-counts: both teams' points-per-game already includes the points they conceded to each other's quality of opponent. A regression on game-level totals would replace it.

S-11 · Add a weather and venue layer

Effort M · Impact 2 · Priority P2 · Decision: ______ Wind matters more than temperature for totals and passing props, and the Week 10 international game in Munich is a genuine venue outlier with no home crowd advantage in the usual sense. Needs an official venue identifier per game and a weather source. Low priority relative to S-01 to S-04, but it is a real omission for totals work.

C. Decision support and the site

S-12 · Add a per-decision "what would change my mind" panel

Effort S · Impact 4 · Priority P1 · Decision: ______ Each analysis page should state the specific observation that would falsify its conclusion. This is the cheapest available defence against confirmation bias, and it converts the site from a report into a decision aid. Example: on the Luck Table, "if Tampa Bay's point differential is driven by one or two outlier games, the unlucky label is an artefact - check the margin distribution."

S-13 · Surface the provenance tier as a site-wide filter

Effort M · Impact 4 · Priority P1 · Decision: ______ Every value already carries VERIFIED, DERIVED, MODELED or UNAVAILABLE. Adding a control that dims or hides everything below a chosen tier would let a reviewer see, in one click, exactly which conclusions rest on verified data and which rest on an assumption. This directly serves the "improve decision making" goal and makes the discipline visible rather than merely documented.

S-14 · Ratify the reward ladder

Effort S · Impact 3 · Priority P2 · Decision: ______ The four-tier ladder in src/nfl/leaderboard.py - promote to live paper trading, extended validation, watchlist, retire - is marked PROPOSAL. It needs an owner decision on what a reward concretely means: bankroll allocation, promotion to live tracking, or something else entirely. The scoring formula is also worth reviewing: ranking on a t-statistic rather than raw ROI means sample size enters the score directly, which is intentional, but the stability and capacity multipliers are judgement calls.

S-15 · Name a governance owner and a review cadence

Effort S · Impact 3 · Priority P2 · Decision: ______ Gate failures currently retire a strategy automatically, but nobody owns the decision to override a gate, and there is no scheduled review. Propose: a weekly review of the leaderboard and the Flagged page, with any gate override recorded in COORDINATION.md together with the reason. An override that is not written down is indistinguishable from a bug.

S-16 · Add an explicit "no bet" default to the origination board

Effort S · Impact 4 · Priority P1 · Decision: ______ The Week 10 board already publishes rating gaps and leaves spreads blank. It should go further and state the recommended action for each game, which today is uniformly no action possible because no market line exists to compare against. Making the default action explicit, rather than implied by a blank cell, removes any chance that a blank is read as a pass or as an approval.

Effort S · Impact 3 · Priority P2 · Decision: ______ Show what changed since the previous run - new injury entries, status changes, newly confirmed absences. A dashboard that shows only the current state hides the thing that is most decision-relevant, which is movement. Depends on S-04 and S-05.

D. Risk and integrity

S-18 · Adopt a written staking policy before any money is involved

Effort S · Impact 5 · Priority P0 · Decision: ______ No bankroll, unit size, maximum exposure or cross-wager correlation limit is defined anywhere in this repository, and the system deliberately does not assume one. A written policy - fractional Kelly with a hard per-wager and per-slate cap, plus a stop-loss rule - should exist and be owner-approved before the first wager is placed, including a paper wager, because a paper-trading record with no staking rule produces a number that cannot be interpreted.

S-19 · Make the placebo test mandatory rather than advisory

Effort S · Impact 4 · Priority P1 · Decision: ______ Gate G5 already requires a permutation placebo p ≤ 0.05. It should additionally require that the same strategy code, run on sign-flipped returns, produces no edge - which catches the commonest failure mode, where an edge is real in the code path but is actually an artefact of how the sample was assembled. Cheap to add, and it is the difference between "passed a test" and "survived an attempt to break it".

S-20 · Add a pre-registration step for every new strategy

Effort M · Impact 4 · Priority P1 · Decision: ______ Require each strategy to record its hypothesis, its inputs and its success criteria before it is evaluated, in a file that is committed and never edited afterwards. This is the only reliable defence against the garden of forking paths: with 21 signals and 13 strategies, the number of implicit comparisons is large enough that something will look significant by chance, and post-hoc rationalisation is invisible in the results. The correlation screen already controls for this within a single screen; pre-registration controls for it across time.

S-21 · Track every rejected hypothesis, not just the survivors

Effort S · Impact 3 · Priority P2 · Decision: ______ A published archive of what was tried and failed is more valuable than a list of what works, because it stops the same dead end being re-explored. Tier 4 of the reward ladder already archives rejections with a reason; this proposes making that archive a first-class page on the site.

S-22 · Confirm the legal position before any deployment

Effort S · Impact 5 · Priority P0 · Decision: ______ The system has no idea what jurisdiction its owner is in and makes no attempt to guess. Whether sports wagering is legal there, whether any intended operator is licensed there, and whether automated collection from each data source complies with that source's terms of use are all owner questions that must be answered before deployment. Everything in this repository should be treated as research until they are.
