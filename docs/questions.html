Q-007

How many bets are needed before a strategy can be called skilful rather than lucky?

AnsweredStatistics

Sample size is a function of the edge and the odds, not a fixed number. The register specifies the calculation rather than asserting a number.

What we know
The calculation is standard: for a binomial record at price p, the number of bets n needed to detect edge e at 95% confidence scales with p(1-p)/e-squared. Because e is small, n is large - typically in the low thousands at standard -110 prices.
What is missing
The realised edge of any of our strategies, which is unknown until backtested.
Evidence
Statistical methodology, not an empirical claim. No NFL-specific number is asserted.
Source
Nevada Gaming Control Board — Gaming Revenue Report
