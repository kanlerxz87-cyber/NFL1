Originating our own linesDesign, not results

What 'our own line' means here. The model produces a probability. That probability is converted to a fair price in the market's own format. That fair price is our line. Only then do we look at the market: an edge exists where our fair price is better than the de-vigged market price by more than the cost of doing business. Comparing a raw market price to a model probability without removing the margin first is the standard way to manufacture a fake edge.

Why
Profit is not a skill measure over any sample we can realistically obtain. CLV is observable immediately after each bet and is not contaminated by result variance. Calibration is used as a tiebreak so that a strategy cannot win by simply agreeing with the market on easy games.

Anti-gaming rules

  • Strategy versions are immutable once registered (S-010).
  • The holdout window is scored once and never re-tuned afterwards.
  • Every bet is logged at placement time with the price captured at that moment.
  • All strategies are corrected for multiple comparisons across the board, because a leaderboard of many strategies guarantees some will look good by chance.
  • Sequential peeking is not permitted: a strategy cannot be declared skilful the moment it crosses a threshold. Evaluation points are fixed in advance.

Rewards (Awaiting owner decision (Q-013))

  • Tier 1 - Top CLV, minimum bets met: promoted to live staking with the largest allocation.
  • Tier 2 - Positive CLV with a confidence interval excluding zero: promoted to live staking with a reduced allocation.
  • Tier 3 - Passes G0-G4 but not G5: stays in research, no allocation.
  • Fail - Any gate failed: returned to research.

Rewards are deliberately tied to the gates, not to profit, so that the incentive is to find skill rather than to find variance.

Removing luck from the equation

Luck is removed by measuring the decision, not the outcome. A bet can be correct and lose. The only measurable evidence of skill available before a long results sample exists is whether the bettor consistently obtained a better price than the market's final price.
