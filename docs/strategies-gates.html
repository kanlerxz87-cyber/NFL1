A gate whose evidence does not exist returns BLOCKED, and BLOCKED is fatal - it is never treated as a pass. That is what makes the current leaderboard empty rather than optimistically populated.

G1Data provenanceEvery input tier is VERIFIED or DERIVED from an official source. A MODELED input may not drive a stake without explicit human sign-off recorded on the Suggestions page.

G6Vig-adjusted edgeEdge must survive at POSTED prices, not only at de-vigged fair prices. Beating the fair price while losing to the vig is not an edge.
G7Stability / no single-source dependencesign of edge retained in >= 70% of leave-one-out folds and no fold contributes more than 35% of total profit
G8Correlation control (multi-leg bets)for parlays and prop combos, every leg pair's correlation must be MEASURED and priced. Independent-product pricing is forbidden when |rho| > 0.15.

The gate engine is exercised in the test suite against a deliberately well-formed hypothetical strategy, so it is demonstrably capable of returning APPROVED. It also rejects a strategy with lookahead, one whose edge is within noise, and one whose profit comes from a single fold. A gate that can only ever say no would be worthless as evidence.
