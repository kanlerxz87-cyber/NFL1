# Leaderboard competition rules

Three tracks (single bet, parlay, player prop combo). Rewards are non-monetary badges; values are blank because no official source defines them. No rank or score is published until all 13 gates pass.

| ID | Rule | Detail | Official source | Verified |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LB-01 | Three tracks: Single bet, Parlay, Player prop combo | Track definitions follow the official bet-type terminology. | [S21](https://nj.gov/oag/ge/2024news/2020sportswagerreportrevised121523.pdf) | yes |
| LB-02 | Eligibility | A strategy appears on the ranked leaderboard only after passing G01-G13. | [S18](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda353.htm) | yes |
| LB-03 | Scoring | Out-of-sample score; numeric formula and weights are blank and flagged until pre-registered. | [S17](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/glossary.htm) | NO — flagged |
| LB-04 | Freeze | Evaluation windows and pre-registration hashes are frozen before scoring. | [S17](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/glossary.htm) | yes |
| LB-05 | Rewards | Non-monetary badges: Verified Signal, Top Single Bet, Top Parlay, Top Player Prop Combo. Reward values are blank and flagged. | [S24](https://github.com/kanlerxz87-cyber/NFL1) | NO — flagged |
| LB-06 | Risk posture | Parlay strategies carry a stricter gate because official state data shows higher loss rates for parlays. | [S21](https://nj.gov/oag/ge/2024news/2020sportswagerreportrevised121523.pdf) | yes |
| LB-07 | Publication | Ranks, scores and sample sizes are published only when verified; otherwise the cells are blank. | [S02](https://www.nfl.com/standings/league/2026/REG) | yes |
