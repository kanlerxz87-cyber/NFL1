A generic Football Operations landing page is not evidence of a particular result or market.

2. **Do not map weather to the stats page.** `pages/evidence.html` links its Weather / venue / conditions row to `https://www.nfl.com/stats/`, which is not a weather source. No approved official weather source is selected in the repository. Leave the weather value/source blank and flagged until one is selected and verified; the official schedule can support venue/date, not weather values. [NFL stats](https://www.nfl.com/stats/) · [NFL schedule](https://www.nfl.com/schedules/2026/by-week/week-10)

## Strategy design recommended for the next implementation

These are **testable design proposals, not measured NFL edges**. There are no prices, model outputs, or performance values to populate yet.

| Strategy family | Signal to test | Minimum evidence before a result can be shown | Official data reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-game spread / total / moneyline | Build an opponent-adjusted distribution for game margin and total points from time-stamped historical game statistics; compare the model probability with a time-aligned, de-vigged sportsbook quote. Publish a line only when the probability advantage remains after price and uncertainty. | Historical game-level features/results, the exact offered price and timestamp, settlement rules, a frozen model version, and a forward holdout. No quote history is present now. | [NFL stats](https://www.nfl.com/stats/) · [official betting-data distribution announcement](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-draftkings-fanduel-fanatics-sports-betting-partners) |
| Player props | Model opportunity first (participation / routes / targets / carries where licensed and available), then the stat distribution conditional on player role, opponent, and dated availability. Compare the distribution with the actual market line and price. | Dated official injury report, verified participation/role records, player game stats, market line and price history, and a leakage-safe holdout. The current snapshot does not contain those role or odds fields. | [NFL injury report](https://www.nfl.com/injuries/league/2026/reg10) · [NFL stats](https://www.nfl.com/stats/) · [NFL Next Gen Stats](https://operations.nfl.com/game-operations-logistics/technology/performance-tracking-data-next-gen-stats) |
| Parlays / player-prop combinations | Estimate or simulate the **joint** outcome distribution; compare it with the exact offered parlay price. Do not multiply marginal probabilities unless independence is supported. A pairwise correlation matrix alone is not sufficient for higher-order joint probabilities. | Aligned per-game outcomes, all legs and prices, explicit settlement/void rules, and uncertainty for the joint estimate. No correlation or joint-outcome values exist now. | [NFL stats](https://www.nfl.com/stats/) · [NFL game center/gamebook example](https://www.nfl.com/games/commanders-at-giants-2026-reg-10) |
| Injury / replacement adjustment | Separate offense and defense; link a dated absence to the actual replacement's position and observed participation. Estimate incremental effects against a pre-specified baseline; do not assign an impact from “starter” or “backup” labels alone. | A stable, dated role source, injury-report chronology, actual participation/game results, and contemporaneous market quotes. The requested 2nd/3rd/4th-string hierarchy is not verified in the current data. | [NFL injury report](https://www.nfl.com/injuries/league/2026/reg10) · [NFL team rosters](https://www.nfl.com/teams/) · [NFL Next Gen Stats](https://operations.nfl.com/game-operations-logistics/technology/performance-tracking-data-next-gen-stats) |

For correlation reporting, retain the observation definition and sample count, use an outcome-appropriate statistic (for example, Pearson correlation for continuous outcomes or phi for binary outcomes), show uncertainty, and condition/stratify by relevant game/role context. Estimate the full joint outcome when evaluating parlays. Until a timestamped dataset exists, leave coefficients blank and flag them **not calculated**. Randomness cannot be removed; leaderboard eligibility should be prospective and auditable rather than based on raw win rate alone.
