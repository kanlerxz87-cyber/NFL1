F-001

No official NFL depth chart exists

HighData gap needs review
Detail
The brief asks the system to know when 2nd, 3rd or 4th string players replace starters. The league does not publish depth charts. A direct fetch of https://www.nfl.com/teams/kansas-city-chiefs/depth-chart returned the NFL's 404 page ('404 - Flag on the Play'). Depth order must therefore be derived, and any derived order is an estimate, not an official fact.
Impact
Blocks REQ-010, REQ-011, REQ-012 as literally specified.
Suggested action
Decide on the snap-share inference approach (suggestion S-007).
Source
NFL.com — Official Injury Report (Week 10, 2026)
F-002

The NFL publishes no official betting lines or odds

HighData gap needs review
Detail
No price appears anywhere on nfl.com. The league's own exclusive data distributor for sports-betting data is Genius Sports, so the only league-authorised route to official betting data is a commercial agreement with them. Without it, every market comparison on this project depends on a non-official price source.
Impact
Blocks market comparison, CLV measurement and therefore every validation gate.
Suggested action
Decide whether to license a feed, or run the leaderboard on an explicitly non-official price source with that caveat published.
Source
Genius Sports — NFL exclusive official data & Watch & Bet distribution partner
F-003

Official injury report: body-part column is empty for 97.6% of rows

HighFeed defect needs review
Detail
Of the 254 players listed on the official Week 10 report, only 6 carry any text in the Injuries column (Patrick - Concussion, Yankoff - Knee, Harrison - Groin, Ben Brown - Knee, Carlton Davis III - Knee, Henderson - Illness). The remaining 248 rows are blank. Counted directly from the ingested official data.
Impact
Any signal that keys off injury type (for example, hamstring versus ankle) cannot be built from this feed as published.
Suggested action
Look for the body-part detail in another official surface, or drop injury-type signals from scope.
Source
NFL.com — Official Injury Report (Week 10, 2026)
F-004

Seahawks show 'No Injuries Reported' while NFL.com news reports their starting QB ruled out

HighFeed inconsistency needs review
Detail
The official Week 10 injury report shows 'No Injuries Reported' for Seattle. The official NFL.com news rail on the same page carries the headline 'Sam Darnold ruled out for Seahawks' Week 10 game vs. Raiders; Drew Lock expected to start again'. A player moved to injured reserve leaves the injury report entirely, so the report understates unavailability for teams with IR placements.
Impact
Injury tracking built on the injury report alone will miss exactly the most severe cases. Directly undermines REQ-009.
Suggested action
Add the official transactions feed (IR / PUP / NFI / elevations) as a mandatory companion to the injury report.
Source
NFL.com — News (lineup / injury news)

F-007

Injury-report definitions and deadlines could not be confirmed on any official domain

MediumVerification needs review
Detail
The categories themselves (DNP / Limited / Full and Out / Doubtful / Questionable) were observed directly on the official report. Their written definitions and the filing deadlines were NOT: operations.nfl.com/gameday/nfl-game-status returned 404, operations.nfl.com/inside-football-ops/health-and-safety/injury-report returned 404, and nfl.com/injuries/inactives returned 404. The commonly repeated definitions and deadlines are therefore published on this site as 'not verified', never as fact.
Impact
We cannot attach an official probability to a designation, which several modelling approaches would want.
Suggested action
Locate the policy text on an official domain, or estimate the mapping empirically from our own history once we have it.
Source
NFL Football Operations

F-010

Every correlation value is blank

HighUnverified needs review
Detail
The brief asks us to determine correlation values. No league-official, government-official or regulator-official source publishes a player-prop correlation matrix, and no historical joint-outcome data has been ingested. The correlation register therefore states the structural direction of each relationship and leaves the magnitude cell empty and flagged.
Impact
Parlays and prop combos cannot be priced honestly yet. This is the honest state, not a gap to be papered over with invented numbers.
Suggested action
Obtain historical joint outcomes, then estimate and hold out (suggestion S-006).
Source
NFL Next Gen Stats

F-013

Los Angeles Rams abbreviated LA on main but LAR here

LowCross-session needs review
Detail
Cross-verification against main's independently transcribed data/verified/standings_2026.csv found 32 of 32 clubs matching exactly on wins, losses, ties, points for, points against, net points, percentage and streak. The single difference is the abbreviation: main uses LA, this session uses LAR for the Los Angeles Rams. The official NFL.com standings page uses the LA logo token in its URLs. This is a naming-convention drift between sessions, not a data error.
Impact
Joins across the two sessions' datasets will drop the Rams silently unless the key is normalised.
Suggested action
Normalise all 32 club abbreviations to one canonical set (official NFL abbreviations) and re-key both datasets.
