Source register

Every value on this site traces to one of these. The tiering is what makes the 'no hallucinations' rule structural rather than aspirational.

  • Tier 1 — league-official or government-official. Published by the league itself or by a government body. May populate any value.
  • Tier 2 — primary issuer or regulated operator. A company's own announcement about its own contract, or a regulator's own filing. May populate a value, but is a claim by an interested party about itself.
  • Tier 3 — community. Open-source redistributions. May be referenced; may never populate a value without re-verification against a Tier 1 source.
Sources marked NOT READ are listed because they are the right candidate for a future input, not because data was taken from them. No value anywhere on this site comes from a source marked NOT READ.
