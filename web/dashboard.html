Why the own lines are not presented as edges

The model was fitted on 2024 and tested on games it never saw. On the 137 games of 2026 it is 9.95 points of mean absolute error against 9.45 for the closing line. The 95% interval on the difference is [-0.032, 1.036].

Fitting the actual margin on both the closing line and the model line, then testing on 2026, puts a weight of 1.336 on the market and -0.328 on the model; hybrid error 9.51 against 9.45 for the market alone. A weight at or below zero means the model's disagreement with the price carried no information in the test window.

The line-sign convention is measured rather than assumed: across 5592 games the correlation between the mirrored closing line and the home team's actual margin is 0.439, so a positive number means the home team is favoured. source: nflverse schedules ↗

Decide now, or wait for information?

How long before skill can be separated from luck?

Before any reward is attached to a strategy, the sample has to be large enough that a real edge would not be mistaken for luck. Using a two-sided test at 5% significance with 80% power, a -110 price and a per-bet standard deviation of 0.95 units:

True edgeBets needed2026 seasons
1% win-probability edge17,68965.0
2% win-probability edge4,42316.3
3% win-probability edge1,9667.2
5% win-probability edge7082.6

Arithmetic from stated assumptions, not a fitted result. A single 272-game season cannot separate a 1-3% edge from noise, which is why the leaderboard currently ranks on the lower bound of a 95% interval and awards zero to anything under the sample minimum. Method: NIST sample-size procedure ↗
