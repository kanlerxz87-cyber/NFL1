What is deliberately left blank

Some values cannot be published because no official or licensed source publishes them. They are not zero, not estimated, and not rounded — they are blank, and they are listed here so nobody mistakes a gap for a finding:

  • Book-level odds — the NFL publishes no betting lines and no licensed feed is connected. Market numbers on this site are consensus closing numbers from the nflverse mirror.
  • Player prop prices — same reason, so no prop edge exists to compute.
  • Who physically starts — the league publishes no depth chart. Only club pages do, and only two were transcribed this week.
  • Leaderboard ranks and rewards — blank until a strategy passes all eight gates; the reward table is paper-only until legal review.
