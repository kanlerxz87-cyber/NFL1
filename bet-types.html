Design consequence

Official data shows parlays (not in-game) losing 86% of the time in the 2019 New Jersey sample, versus 51–53% for several straight-bet types. Combined with the NIST requirement to measure rather than assume relationships between variables, this is why parlay and player-prop-combo strategies carry an additional leg-correlation gate (G09) and why no parlay strategy is eligible for the leaderboard yet.
