What cannot be answered yet

  • Did the projected backup actually play, and how many snaps? — missing: official inactive lists for Week 10 and official snap counts. Gate: capture the official inactive list 90 minutes before kickoff and an official snap-count source.
  • Is an absent player healthy or on reserve? — missing: an official reserve/injured-reserve list capture. Gate: capture the official transactions log and join it to the weekly report.
  • Is the depth chart current? — missing: a date on the chart (30 of 31 carry none). Gate: a dated official source per club, or a roster-event version stamp (the brief's PR-style requirement).
  • What is the injury surprise rather than the injury level? — missing: two or more dated captures of the same report to difference. Gate: daily Wednesday-to-kickoff captures with a retained hash; the repository has 2 capture days and 0 recorded designation changes.

Why these limits exist

  • No inactive list, Game Book or snap count for any 2026 game, so "played" is unobservable (0 of 137 played games archived).
  • 30 of 31 depth charts carry no date, so a chart cannot be versioned against a roster event.
  • 14 of 31 charts carry the club's own "unofficial" label. The label is carried through rather than dropped.
  • Absence from the weekly report is not availability. A reserve-list player is not listed at all, and no reserve-list capture exists, so "not listed" is published as "not listed".
