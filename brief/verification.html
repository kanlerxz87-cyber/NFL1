  • The 32-club depth charts themselves were not re-fetched; this sub-site uses the committed ladder and only checks its arithmetic and its internal consistency.
  • Roof and surface were not captured from an official source and are left blank wherever a page would otherwise show them.
  • Reserve/injured-reserve transactions named in the brief (for example Malik Willis) were not checked against an official transactions log in this session.
  • The Backtest Archive, the Strategy Registry and the Decision Room were not re-verified; they are linked, not restated.
  • The third-party price source was not checked against any official price (none exists).

How these pages gate themselves

  • brief/verify.py fails the build when a table row has no https source link and no explicit "not published" label.
  • It fails when any table cell renders empty, or contains one of the usual stand-in tokens — the four-letter to-do and not-applicable markers, the sample-text word, and a literal empty-value repr.
  • It fails when a local href does not resolve, or when a page is unreachable from the index.
  • It fails when a data/session-2b927826/*.json file changes without the pages being rebuilt.
  • The repository's existing gates (tools/verify.py, check_reachability.py, build_site_all.py --check) run over these pages too, unchanged.
