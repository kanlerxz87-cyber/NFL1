The missing information, ranked by what it unblocks

  • Official inactive lists — one fetch per game window — turn "a backup probably started" into "a backup played". Nothing else moves so many rows for so little work.
  • Official Game Books, one per played game, give the actual starters and close Q08 and Q15.
  • Daily injury captures — the workflow already exists — produce the first designation change the readiness gate is waiting for.
  • An opening and a closing price per market is the only route to closing-line value, and it cannot be backfilled: every week without it is lost permanently.
  • A terms review decides whether the row-level tables that already exist may stay public.

What a decision needs that no capture can supply

  • Which pre-registration registry is authoritative — four exist; a person must choose.
  • Whether a third-party price is acceptable — the league publishes none, so this is a policy call with a real consequence for every published number.
  • The operator's void and push rules for every market — they are the operator's, not the NFL's, and a parlay cannot be ranked without them.
  • How long a live sample must run before a demotion is informative — the power table says roughly seven seasons at a 3-point-equivalent edge, which may be longer than the project's patience.
