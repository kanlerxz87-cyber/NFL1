Stage 1 — does the model forecast better than the closing line?

Brier skill = 1 − Brier(model)/Brier(benchmark). A negative value means the benchmark is better. The test statistic is the lower bound of a game-cluster bootstrap interval, not the point estimate. The benchmark for the side and the total is the closing number at p = 0.5; for the moneyline it is the de-vigged closing price, which is a stronger benchmark than a coin flip.

Parlays and combos — the joint-evidence gate

Multiplying marginal probabilities is invalid under dependence, so a combo may only be ranked when a directly validated joint probability and the operator's void and push rules are both available.

parlays
TestedSurvive BH at 5%Void/push rules recordedGateSource
Every two-leg pair in the scored set66 not recorded SHUT — 1 of 66 pairs survives the multiplicity charge, and no combo is ranked until the rules are recorded and the joint probability is validated directly
Why a correlated parlay is not a product. The product of two legs is what a book would pay on a standard parlay. Because the legs are correlated, that price is only fair if the correlation is zero; the correlation_ratio column shows how far from zero it is.
