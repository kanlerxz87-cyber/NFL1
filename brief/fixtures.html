Why roof and surface stay blank. The pinned feed carries both, and it is a reasonable third-party parse, but publishing it under an official label is exactly the error the brief's verification rule exists to prevent. A stadium's roof does not change between games, so this can be sourced once per venue from a club or league page and never again — that is suggestion SG-08.
