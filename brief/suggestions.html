If you do only three things

  • SG-03 — widen the injury capture. The workflow exists; it needs a schedule. It produces the first designation change this repository has ever recorded, which is the gate on the brief's central injury requirement.
  • SG-01 and SG-02 — capture the inactive list and the Game Book. Together they turn a projection into an observation and close the availability graph.
  • SG-04 — write the price policy and start the opening/closing store. It cannot be backfilled, so every week without it is a permanent gap in closing-line value.

What not to do next

  • Do not model availability with a fixed points penalty. The brief forbids it and the honest version of the test already exists (SG-09).
  • Do not rank anything on this week's numbers. Nothing clears the gates and every result is inside its luck band (SG-10).
  • Do not fill a blank from the third-party feed. Roof and surface are the current temptation; they are blank for a reason (SG-08).
  • Do not build a 3rd/4th-string deficiency matrix yet. No club is projected to start one this week, so the matrix would have no rows and would look like a defect.
